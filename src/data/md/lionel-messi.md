# Lionel Messi

**Inter Miami & Argentina | 8 Ballon d'Or Awards | World Cup Champion 2022**

Lionel Messi is widely considered the greatest footballer of all time, having spent the majority of his career at Barcelona before joining Inter Miami in 2023. His combination of vision, passing ability, dribbling, and goal-scoring has redefined what is possible in the sport, earning him a record eight Ballon d'Or awards and leading Argentina to World Cup glory in 2022.

## Early Life in Rosario

Lionel Andrés Messi was born on June 24, 1987, in Rosario, Argentina. He began playing football at age four, joining local club Grandoli coached by his father, Jorge Messi. At age eight, he joined Rosario Central's youth academy, where he demonstrated extraordinary talent but was diagnosed with a growth hormone deficiency that threatened his football career. At age thirteen, Messi moved to Barcelona's academy in Spain, leaving his family and homeland behind to pursue his dream of becoming a professional footballer. The club paid for his medical treatment, and his rapid development soon silenced any doubts about his potential.

## Barcelona Era: The Greatest Club Career in History

Messi spent twenty-one years at Barcelona, from the youth academy through the first team, becoming the club's all-time leading scorer with 672 goals in 778 appearances. He won thirty-five trophies with the club, including ten La Liga titles and four UEFA Champions League crowns. His legendary partnership with Andrés Iniesta and Xavi Hernández created one of football's greatest eras, with Barcelona winning multiple trebles and dominating European football. Messi's famous rivalry with Cristiano Ronaldo defined an era of the sport, pushing both players to unprecedented heights.

## Individual Records and Achievements

Messi's individual achievements seem designed to be broken by no one. He has won a record eight Ballon d'Or awards, seven FIFA World Player of the Year/Ballon d'Or runner-up honors, and countless scoring records. His 91 goals in 2012 stood as the calendar-year record for over forty years. He won the UEFA Champions League top scorer award six times and La Liga's top scorer award eight times. His passing ability, vision, and creativity are considered his signature gifts, allowing him to create chances for teammates even when not scoring—a combination that makes him uniquely valuable to any team.

## World Cup 2022 and International Career

For years, Messi's international career appeared cursed. Argentina lost four consecutive finals between 2004 and 2016, including the 2014 World Cup final to Germany and the 2016 Copa America to Chile. However, at age thirty-five, Messi finally achieved his life's ambition in Qatar 2022. He led Argentina to victory in the World Cup, defeating France in one of the greatest finals in history. Messi scored twice in the match and converted his penalty in the shootout, finally lifting the trophy that had eluded him. He retired from international football after the 2022 tournament, though returned for the 2024 Copa America.

## Move to Inter Miami and Legacy

In July 2023, Messi joined Inter Miami in Major League Soccer, citing the opportunity to enjoy football without pressure and to grow the sport in the United States. He instantly transformed the club's fortunes, leading them to their first trophy, the Leagues Cup, in his very first game with a stoppage-time free kick. His arrival in MLS dramatically increased attendance and television viewership across the league. At thirty-seven years old, Messi's continued excellence—winning the 2023 Ballon d'Or at age thirty-six—demonstrates that genius can persist well into what traditional athletes consider the twilight of careers.
