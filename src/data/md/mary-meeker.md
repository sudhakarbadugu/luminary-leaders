# Mary Meeker — The Queen of the Internet

## 1. THE HOOK

In 1995, she published the first Internet Trends Report. She continued for 25 years. The report became the industry's most anticipated publication. She tracked global internet users, mobile adoption, e-commerce growth. She worked at Morgan Stanley as a technology analyst. She covered Netscape, Amazon, Google, eBay. She was called "Queen of the Internet" by Barron's in 1998. She joined Kleiner Perkins in 2010. She invested in Facebook, Twitter, Slack, Spotify, DocuSign. She founded Bond Capital in 2019. Her famous quote: "The internet changes everything."

How does an analyst from Indiana become the Queen of the Internet?

## 2. ORIGIN — THE "Before" (The Relatability Engine)

Mary Meeker was born in September 1959 in Portland, Indiana. She studied at DePauw University (BA) and Cornell University (MBA).

She was running from conventional finance. She saw the internet before others. She understood its potential.

## 3. THE FIRE — Failures, Rock-Bottom & Turning Points

### Morgan Stanley (1980s-2010)

Meeker worked as a technology analyst at Morgan Stanley. She covered Netscape, Amazon, Google, eBay.

### Internet Trends Report (1995-2019)

In 1995, Meeker published the first Internet Trends Report. It became an annual ritual. The report tracked global internet users, mobile adoption, e-commerce, digital advertising.

### Kleiner Perkins (2010-2019)

In 2010, Meeker joined Kleiner Perkins as a partner. She invested in Facebook, Twitter, Slack, Spotify, DocuSign.

### Bond Capital (2019-Present)

In 2019, Meeker founded Bond Capital. She built her own firm.

## 4. THE GRIND — The Invisible Years (The Method)

Meeker's method is defined by data-driven analysis, long-term trend identification, and transparency.

### Follow the Data

Meeker's famous maxim: "Follow the data." She does not chase hype. She grounds analysis in metrics.

### Long-Term Trends

Meeker asks: "What does this mean for the next 10 years?" She thinks in decades, not quarters.

### Transparency

Meeker's slides are evidence, not arguments. Viewers can see the data. They can reach their own conclusions.

The invisible years—the years of building the Internet Trends Report—were years of data compilation. Meeker did not guess. She measured.

## 5. THE TEST — The Breakthrough Moment
