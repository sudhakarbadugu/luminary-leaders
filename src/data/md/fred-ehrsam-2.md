Ehrsam left Coinbase in 2018. He wanted to focus on Paradigm. Armstrong took full control. Ehrsam's departure was amicable but significant.

### Paradigm (2018-Present)

Ehrsam founded Paradigm—a crypto-focused investment firm. The fund invested in Coinbase, Bitcoi, Ethereum, and dozens of other crypto companies. Paradigm grew to $10B+ in assets under management.
