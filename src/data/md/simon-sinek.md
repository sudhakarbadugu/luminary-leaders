# Simon Sinek — The Optimist Who Starts With Why

## 1. THE HOOK

In 2009, he gave a TED talk called "How Great Leaders Inspire Action." It went viral. 60M+ views. One of the most-watched TED talks ever. He wrote "Start With Why." He created the Golden Circle theory. He says: "People don't buy what you do; they buy why you do it."

How does an advertising executive become a leadership philosopher?

## 2. ORIGIN — THE "Before" (The Relatability Engine)

Simon Sinek was born October 9, 1973, in Wimbledon, London, England. He is British-American. He studied at Brandeis University (BA anthropology), City University of London (MA).

He was running from advertising. He saw inspiration. He wanted to understand leadership.

## 3. THE FIRE — Failures, Rock-Bottom & Turning Points

### Advertising Executive

Sinek worked in advertising. He lost his passion. He felt empty.

### Start With Why (2009)

In 2009, Sinek wrote "Start With Why." The Golden Circle: Why, How, What. Leaders inspire by starting with Why.

### TEDxPuget Sound 2009

In 2009, Sinek gave a TED talk: "How Great Leaders Inspire Action." It went viral. 60M+ views. Global recognition.

### Leaders Eat Last (2014)

In 2014, Sinek wrote "Leaders Eat Last." Trust. Safety. Teams.

### Together Is Better (2016)

In 2016, Sinek wrote "Together Is Better." Leadership stories.

### Find Your Why (2017)

In 2017, Sinek wrote "Find Your Why." Practical workbook.

### The Infinite Game (2019)

In 2019, Sinek wrote "The Infinite Game." Finite vs infinite games. Business is infinite.

### A Bit of Optimism Podcast

Sinek hosts "A Bit of Optimism" podcast. Conversations on leadership.

## 4. THE GRIND — The Invisible Years (The Method)

Sinek's method is defined by Golden Circle, trust, and infinite game thinking.

### Golden Circle

Sinek's philosophy: Start with Why. Why inspires. People buy why.

### Trust

Sinek's philosophy: "A team is not a group of people who work together. A team is a group of people who trust each other." Trust first.

### Infinite Game

Sinek's philosophy: Business is infinite. Finite players focus on winning. Infinite players focus on continuing.

The invisible years—the years of advertising—taught Sinek what he didn't want. He became a leadership philosopher.

## 5. THE TEST — THE BREAKTHROUGH MOMENT
