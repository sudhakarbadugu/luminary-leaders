# Travis Kalanick — The Aggressive CEO Who Disrupted Transportation and Was Forced Out

## 1. THE HOOK

In 2009, he cofounded Uber. He grew it to 3 billion rides. In 2017, he was forced to resign after scandals. Toxic workplace. #deleteUber. He founded CloudKitchens in 2018. He says: "Every problem has a solution. You just have to find it."

How does a disruptor get forced out of his own company?

## 2. ORIGIN — THE "Before" (The Relatability Engine)

Travis Kalanick was born August 18, 1976, in Los Angeles, California. He studied at University of California, Los Angeles (UCLA, BS computer science 2001).

He was running from traditional software. He saw disruption. He wanted to challenge industries.

## 3. THE FIRE — Failures, Rock-Bottom & Turning Points

### Scour (1999-2000)

In 1999, Kalanick founded Scour. File-sharing. Sued by record labels. Bankrupt in 2000.

### Red Swoosh (2001-2007)

In 2001, Kalanick founded Red Swoosh. Distributed content delivery. Worked.

### Uber (2009-2017)

In 2009, Kalanick cofounded Uber with Garrett Camp. Ride-sharing. 3 billion rides.

### Scandals

Toxic workplace. #deleteUber. Sexual harassment. Aggressive culture.

### Forced Out (2017)

In 2017, Kalanick was forced to resign. Investors demanded his exit.

### CloudKitchens (2018-Present)

In 2018, Kalanick founded CloudKitchens. Ghost kitchens.

## 4. THE GRIND — The Invisible Years (The Method)

Kalanick's method is defined by aggressive growth, disruption, and finding solutions.

### Aggressive Growth

Kalanick's philosophy: Grow fast. Disrupt. Win markets.

### Disruption

Kalanick's philosophy: "Competition breeds innovation." Challenge industries.

### Finding Solutions

Kalanick's philosophy: "Every problem has a solution. You just have to find it."

The invisible years—the years after Scour—gave Kalanick resilience. He founded Uber.
