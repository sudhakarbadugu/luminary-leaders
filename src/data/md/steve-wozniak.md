# Steve Wozniak — The Engineer Who Single-Handedly Designed Apple I and II

## 1. THE HOOK

In 1976, he single-handedly designed the Apple I. In 1977, he single-handedly designed the Apple II—the first highly successful mass-produced microcomputer. He cofounded Apple with Steve Jobs. He left Apple in 1985. He taught computer classes to children. He says: "I never wanted money. I wanted fun."

How does an HP engineer design the future of computing?

## 2. ORIGIN — THE "Before" (The Relatability Engine)

Steve Wozniak was born August 11, 1950, in San Jose, California. He studied at University of California, Berkeley (BS computer science 1986—after Apple).

He was running from corporate ambition. He saw electronics. He wanted to design computers.

## 3. THE FIRE — Failures, Rock-Bottom & Turning Points

### HP Engineer

Wozniak worked as an engineer at HP. He designed calculators. He wanted to build computers.

### Apple I (1976)

In 1976, Wozniak single-handedly designed the Apple I. Steve Jobs sold it. Cofounded Apple.

### Apple II (1977)

In 1977, Wozniak single-handedly designed the Apple II. First highly successful mass-produced microcomputer. Revolution.

### Leave Apple (1985)

In 1985, Wozniak left Apple. He wanted to teach. He didn't want corporate battles.

### CL9, Wheels of Zeus

Wozniak founded CL9 (universal remote control), Wheels of Zeus (wireless GPS). Not as successful as Apple.

### Teaching

Wozniak taught computer classes to elementary school children. He loved teaching.

## 4. THE GRIND — The Invisible Years (The Method)

Wozniak's method is defined by engineering genius, hacker ethic, and fun.

### Engineering Genius

Wozniak's philosophy: Single-handedly design. Pure engineering. Art.

### Hacker Ethic

Wozniak's philosophy: Hacker ethic. Share knowledge. Open.

### Fun Over Money

Wozniak's philosophy: "I never wanted money. I wanted fun." Design for joy.

The invisible years—the years at HP—gave Wozniak engineering skills. He designed Apple I and II.

## 5. THE TEST — THE BREAKTHROUGH MOMENT

Apple II is the test. First mass-produced microcomputer. Engineering genius works.

## 6. THE PHILOSOPHY — Stealable Wisdom

**On Trust:**
"Never trust a computer you can't throw out a window."

**On Fun:**
"I never wanted money. I wanted fun."

**On Programming:**
"I think everyone should learn how to program."

## 7. THE LEGACY — Purpose Beyond Profit + Human Side

Steve Wozniak is retired. He teaches. He advocates for engineering education.

### What does he want to be remembered for?

In interviews, Wozniak emphasizes engineering and fun. He wants to inspire engineers.

## 8. FINAL MOTIVATION & PERSONAL APPLICATION Guide

Steve Wozniak's career demonstrates that engineering genius builds empires.

1. **Design single-handedly** — Wozniak designed Apple I and II alone. Pure engineering.

3. **Teach** — Wozniak taught children. Share knowledge.

4. **Share the hacker ethic** — Hacker ethic. Open knowledge.

5. **Don't fight corporate battles** — Wozniak left Apple. Leave when it's not fun.

Engineering genius builds empires.
