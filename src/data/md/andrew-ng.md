# Andrew Ng — The Man Who Democratized AI Education

## 1. THE HOOK

In 2011, he founded Google Brain with Jeff Dean, Greg Corrado, and others. In 2012, he launched the Coursera Machine Learning course. It had 100,000+ students in the first year. He is the cofounder of Coursera. He founded DeepLearning.AI. He was chief scientist at Baidu. He is an adjunct professor at Stanford. He says: "AI is the new electricity."

How does a professor teach AI to 100,000 students in one year?

## 2. ORIGIN — THE "Before" (The Relatability Engine)

Andrew Ng was born in 1976. He is British. He studied at Carnegie Mellon (BS), MIT (MS), UC Berkeley (PhD).

He was running from academic boundaries. He saw that AI could transform every industry. He democratized education.

## 3. THE FIRE — Failures, Rock-Bottom & Turning Points

### Google Brain (2011-2012)

In 2011, Ng founded Google Brain. He was the founding lead. The team included Andrew Dai, Quoc Le, Greg Corrado, Jeff Dean.

### Coursera (2012)

In 2012, Ng cofounded Coursera with Daphne Koller. He launched the Machine Learning course. It had 100,000+ students in the first year.

### DeepLearning.AI

Ng founded DeepLearning.AI. He created the Deep Learning Specialization. He continued democratizing AI education.

### Baidu (2014-2017)

From 2014 to 2017, Ng was chief scientist at Baidu. He led AI research.

### Landing on Helicopter

Ng demonstrated landing a helicopter using reinforcement learning. It was a landmark in AI research.

## 4. THE GRIND — The Invisible Years (The Method)

Ng's method is defined by democratization, education, and transformation.

### Democratizing AI

Ng's philosophy: AI should be accessible. He created online courses. He taught millions.

### AI Transformation of Companies

Ng advocates for AI transformation. He helps companies adopt AI. He wrote about AI transformation playbooks.

### Deep Learning

Ng focused on deep learning. He taught it. He applied it.

The invisible years—the years of teaching and building—gave Ng reach. He taught millions. He transformed industries.

## 5. THE TEST — The Breakthrough Moment

Coursera is the test. 100,000+ students in the first year. Millions of learners. AI education democratized.

## 6. THE PHILOSOPHY — Stealable Wisdom

**On AI:**
"AI is the new electricity."

**On Learning:**
"If you learn deeply about how to do something, you'll have an insight that the person who's just learning shallowly won't have."

**On Transformation:**
"We are entering an era where AI will transform every industry."

## 7. THE LEGACY — Purpose Beyond Profit + Human Side

Andrew Ng is still active. He teaches. He writes. He helps companies.

### What does he want to be remembered for?

In interviews, Ng emphasizes democratization and transformation. He wants AI to benefit everyone.
