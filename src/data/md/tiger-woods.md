# Tiger Woods — The Golfer Who Won 15 Major Championships

## 1. THE HOOK

He won 15 major championships. He held world No. 1 for 683 weeks. He came back from scandal and injuries to win the 2019 Masters. He says: "No matter how good you get, you can always get better."

How does a kid from Cypress dominate golf?

## 2. ORIGIN — THE "Before" (The Relatability Engine)

Tiger Woods was born December 30, 1975, in Cypress, California. Coached by father Earl Woods. Stanford University.

He was running from his father's expectations. He saw golf. He wanted to dominate.

## 3. THE FIRE — Failures, Rock-Bottom & Turning Points

### First Major (1997)

Woods won Masters in 1997 at 21. Dominated.

### Tiger Slam (2000-2001)

Woods held all 4 major trophies at once. Tiger Slam.

### Scandal (2009)

Woods faced infidelity scandal. Lost sponsors. Took break.

### Injuries (2014-2018)

Woods faced back injuries. Multiple surgeries.

### Comeback (2019)

Woods won the 2019 Masters. Emotional comeback.

## 4. THE GRIND — The Invisible Years (The Method)

Woods's method is defined by grinding, improvement, and focus.

### Grind

Woods's philosophy: Grind every shot. Intensity.

### Improvement

### Focus

Woods's philosophy: Extreme focus. Block out everything.
