# Evan Williams — The Medium Founder Who Pioneered Blogging and Twitter

## 1. THE HOOK

In 1999, he founded Pyra Labs—Blogger. In 2003, Google bought it. In 2006, he cofounded Twitter. In 2012, he founded Medium. He pioneered blogging. He pioneered microblogging. He pioneered publishing. He says: "I think the internet is the most important invention since the printing press."

How does a Nebraska dropout pioneer three publishing revolutions?

## 2. ORIGIN — THE "Before" (The Relatability Engine)

Evan Williams was born March 31, 1972, in Clarks, Nebraska. He studied at University of Nebraska-Lincoln (dropped out).

He was running from traditional media. He saw blogging. He wanted to publish.

## 3. THE FIRE — Failures, Rock-Bottom & Turning Points

### Pyra Labs / Blogger (1999)

In 1999, Williams founded Pyra Labs. Blogger—one of first blogging platforms.

### Google Acquisition (2003)

In 2003, Google acquired Pyra Labs. Williams joined Google.

### Twitter (2006)

In 2006, Williams cofounded Twitter with Jack Dorsey, Noah Glass, Biz Stone.

### Twitter CEO (2008-2010)

Williams became Twitter CEO in 2008. He led until 2010.

### Medium (2012-Present)

In 2012, Williams founded Medium. Publishing platform.

## 4. THE GRIND — The Invisible Years (The Method)

Williams' method is defined by publishing platforms, better editor than writer, and internet importance.

### Publishing Platforms

Williams' philosophy: Blogger, Twitter, Medium. Platforms for expression.

### Better Editor

Williams' philosophy: "I'm a better editor than writer." Build platforms, not content.

### Internet Importance

Williams' philosophy: "I think the internet is the most important invention since the printing press."

The invisible years—the years at Google—gave Williams platform experience. He founded Medium.

## 5. THE TEST — THE BREAKTHROUGH MOMENT

Three platforms is the test. Blogger. Twitter. Medium. Publishing revolutions work.
