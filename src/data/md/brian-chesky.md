# Brian Chesky — The Designer Who Built Airbnb on Trust

## 1. THE HOOK

In 2007, he moved to San Francisco. He couldn't afford rent. He rented air mattresses in his apartment. He called it "Airbed and Breakfast." In 2008, he cofounded Airbnb with Joe Gebbia and Nathan Blecharczyk. Y Combinator initially rejected them. Paul Graham said: make "air mattresses on living room floor" sound less weird. In 2020, Airbnb lost 80% of revenue in 8 weeks. He laid off 25%. He wrote a memo about staying true to mission. He says: "Build something that 100 people love, not something that 1 million people kind of like."

How does an industrial designer disrupt hospitality?

## 2. ORIGIN — THE "Before" (The Relatability Engine)

Brian Chesky was born August 24, 1981, in Niskayuna, New York. He studied at Rhode Island School of Design (RISD, BFA industrial design). He met Joe Gebbia at RISD. He moved to San Francisco in 2007.

He was running from traditional design. He saw community. He wanted to build belonging.

## 3. THE FIRE — Failures, Rock-Bottom & Turning Points

### The Idea (2007)

In 2007, Chesky moved to San Francisco. He couldn't afford rent. He rented air mattresses. "Airbed and Breakfast." He and Joe Gebbia hosted guests.

### Y Combinator (2009)

In 2009, Airbnb applied to Y Combinator. Paul Graham rejected them. He told them: make "air mattresses on living room floor" sound less weird. They got in.

### Scaling

Airbnb scaled to 150 million+ travelers. Millions of listings. Disrupted hospitality industry.

### Regulatory Battles

Airbnb faced regulatory battles globally. Hotels, cities fought back. Chesky navigated.

### IPO (2020)

In December 2020, Airbnb IPO'd at $47 billion valuation.

### COVID (2020)

In 2020, Airbnb lost 80% of revenue in 8 weeks. Chesky laid off 25% of employees. He wrote a memo: stay true to mission. He refocused on core values.

## 4. THE GRIND — The Invisible Years (The Method)

Chesky's method is defined by design thinking, 11-star experience, and community trust.

### Design Thinking

Chesky's philosophy: "Design is not just what it looks like. Design is how it works." Industrial design applied to business.

### 11-Star Experience

Chesky's concept: Imagine the perfect experience. Then work backward. Build for love.

### Community Trust

Chesky's philosophy: Trust matters. Host and guest trust. Community building.

The invisible years—the years of renting air mattresses—taught Chesky trust. He built community.

## 5. THE TEST — The Breakthrough Moment

150 million+ travelers is the test. Hospitality disrupted. Design thinking works.
