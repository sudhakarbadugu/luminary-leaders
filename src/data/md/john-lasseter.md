# John Lasseter — The Animator Who Made Pixar a Storytelling Empire

## 1. THE HOOK

In 1986, he cofounded Pixar. He directed Toy Story— the first CGI feature (1995). He won 2 Oscars. He became CCO of Pixar and Disney Animation. He says: "Technology inspires art, and art challenges technology."

How does a fired Disney animator build Pixar?

## 2. ORIGIN — THE "Before" (The Relatability Engine)

John Lasseter was born January 12, 1957, in Los Angeles, California. He studied at California Institute of the Arts (BFA character animation). First student accepted to CalArts character animation program.

He was running from traditional animation. He saw CGI. He wanted to make the first CGI film.

## 3. THE FIRE — Failures, Rock-Bottom & Turning Points

### Disney Animator

Lasseter started at Disney. He pitched The Brave Little Toaster. Disney fired him. They said CGI would never work.

### Lucasfilm (1983-1986)

In 1983, Lasseter joined Lucasfilm Computer Graphics Group. He worked with Ed Catmull.

### Pixar Cofounder (1986)

In 1986, Lasseter cofounded Pixar with Steve Jobs and Ed Catmull. CCO from 1986-2018.

### Toy Story (1995)

In 1995, Lasseter directed Toy Story. First CGI feature. Revolutionary.

### Pixar Films

Lasseter directed A Bug's Life, Toy Story 2, Cars, Cars 2. Executive producer on all Pixar films.

### Disney Acquisition (2006)

In 2006, Disney acquired Pixar for $7.4 billion. Lasseter became CCO of Walt Disney Animation Studios.

### Departure (2018)

In 2018, Lasseter left Pixar and Disney. #MeToo controversies.

## 4. THE GRIND — The Invisible Years (The Method)

Lasseter's method is defined by storytelling, technology, and trust the process.

### Storytelling Is King

Lasseter's philosophy: Story first. Technology serves story. Storytelling is king.

### Technology Inspires Art

Lasseter's philosophy: "Technology inspires art, and art challenges technology." Push boundaries.

### Trust the Process

Lasseter's philosophy: Braintrust. Trust the process. Iterate.

The invisible years—the years at Disney—taught Lasseter animation. He brought it to CGI.

## 5. THE TEST — THE BREAKTHROUGH MOMENT

Toy Story is the test. First CGI feature. $7.4 billion acquisition. Storytelling is king.
