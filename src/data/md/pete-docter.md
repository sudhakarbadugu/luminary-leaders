# Pete Docter — The CCO Who Brings Emotion to Animation

## 1. THE HOOK

In 1990, he joined Pixar as the 8th employee. He directed Monsters Inc, Up, Inside Out, Soul. He won 3 Oscars. In 2018, he became CCO of Pixar, succeeding John Lasseter. He says: "Animation is not about moving drawings. It's about moving audiences."

How does the 8th employee become Pixar's creative leader?

## 2. ORIGIN — THE "Before" (The Relatability Engine)

Pete Docter was born October 10, 1968, in Bloomington, Minnesota. He studied at California Institute of the Arts (BFA character animation).

He was running from traditional animation. He saw Pixar. He wanted to tell emotional stories.

## 3. THE FIRE — Failures, Rock-Bottom & Turning Points

### Pixar (1990-Present)

In 1990, Docter joined Pixar as the 8th employee. Story supervisor on Toy Story, Toy Story 2.

### Monsters Inc (2001)

In 2001, Docter directed Monsters Inc. Monster world. Emotional depth.

### Up (2009)

In 2009, Docter directed Up. The opening sequence. Emotional storytelling. Oscar for Best Animated Feature.

### Inside Out (2015)

In 2015, Docter directed Inside Out. Emotions as characters. Joy, Sadness, Anger, Fear, Disgust. Oscar for Best Animated Feature.

### Soul (2020)

In 2020, Docter directed Soul. Jazz and purpose. Oscar for Best Animated Feature.

### CCO of Pixar (2018-Present)

In 2018, Docter became CCO of Pixar. He succeeded John Lasseter.

## 4. THE GRIND — The Invisible Years (The Method)

Docter's method is defined by emotional depth, trust the process, and ask questions.

### Emotional Depth

Docter's philosophy: Animation moves audiences. Emotion first.

### Trust the Process

Docter's philosophy: Braintrust. Iterate. Trust.

### Ask Questions

Docter's philosophy: "You don't have to have all the answers. You just have to have the willingness to keep asking questions."

The invisible years—the years as story supervisor—taught Docter storytelling. He became Pixar's CCO.

## 5. THE TEST — THE BREAKTHROUGH MOMENT

3 Oscars is the test. Up, Inside Out, Soul. Emotional depth works.
