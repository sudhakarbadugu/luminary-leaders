# Prem Watsa — The Warren Buffett of Canada

## 1. THE HOOK

He is called the "Warren Buffett of Canada." He founded Fairfax Financial Holdings in 1985. He built an insurance company through acquisitions. He uses insurance float to invest. He bet against US housing before the 2008 crash—like John Paulson. He bought BlackBerry in 2013—a controversial move. He holds large cash positions and hedges. He is a value investor. He is patient. He is contrarian. His famous quote: "We're value investors. We buy things when they're out of favor."

How does an immigrant from India become the Warren Buffett of Canada?

## 2. ORIGIN — THE "Before" (The Relatability Engine)

Prem Watsa was born August 5, 1950, in Hyderabad, India. He studied chemical engineering at IIT Madras. He earned an MBA at the University of Western Ontario. He moved to Canada in 1972.

He was running from conventional paths. He could have stayed in India. He chose Canada.

## 3. THE FIRE — Failures, Rock-Bottom & Turning Points

### Fairfax Financial (1985)

In 1985, Watsa founded Fairfax Financial Holdings. He built an insurance company through acquisitions.

### The 2008 Bet

Before the 2008 crash, Watsa bet against US housing. He made billions. He was like John Paulson—contrarian and right.

### BlackBerry (2013)

In 2013, Watsa bought BlackBerry. It was controversial. Some said it was a value trap. Watsa held.

### Performance

Watsa returned approximately 15-20% annualized in early years. More mixed recently, but strong long-term record.

## 4. THE GRIND — The Invisible Years (The Method)

Watsa's method is defined by insurance float, value investing, and contrarianism.

### Insurance Float

Watsa uses insurance float—like Buffett. He receives premiums upfront. He invests the float. He pays claims later.

### Value Investing

Watsa is a value investor. He buys when things are out of favor. He waits for recognition.

### Contrarianism

Watsa bets against the crowd. He bet against housing before 2008. He bought BlackBerry when others sold.

The invisible years—the years of building Fairfax—were years of acquiring insurance companies. Watsa built float.

## 5. THE TEST — The Breakthrough Moment

The 2008 bet was the test. Watsa bet against housing. He was right. He made billions.
