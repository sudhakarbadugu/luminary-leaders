# Benjamin Graham — The Father of Value Investing Who Taught Warren Buffett

## 1. THE HOOK

In 1949, he published a book called "The Intelligent Investor." It is still in print. Warren Buffett calls it "the best book on investing ever written." In 1934, he published "Security Analysis" with David Dodd. It is still read. Benjamin Graham is called the "Father of Value Investing." He invented the concept of "margin of safety"—buying stocks below their intrinsic value. He managed the Graham-Newman fund for nearly three decades. He returned approximately 20% annually. He taught at Columbia University. One of his students was Warren Buffett. Graham died in 1976, but his principles are still taught.

How does a man born in 1894 still influence investors today?

## 2. ORIGIN — The "Before" (The Relatability Engine)

Benjamin Graham was born May 9, 1894, in London. His family immigrated to the United States when he was a child. They settled in New York.

His father died when Graham was young. The family struggled financially. Graham worked hard. He studied at Columbia University. He graduated in 1914. He was offered a teaching position, but he chose Wall Street.

He was running from poverty. His family had lost everything. He wanted financial security. He learned to analyze financial statements.

## 3. THE FIRE — Failures, Rock-Bottom & Turning Points

### The Great Depression

In 1929, the market crashed. Graham lost money. He had been using margin—borrowed money. The losses were significant. He nearly went bankrupt. The experience taught him the importance of margin of safety.

### Graham-Newman Corp

Graham founded the Graham-Newman Corporation. It was a value fund. He bought stocks trading below their intrinsic value. He looked for "net-nets"—stocks trading below their liquidation value. He returned approximately 20% annually over three decades.

### The Intelligent Investor (1949)

In 1949, Graham published "The Intelligent Investor." It became a classic. Warren Buffett read it. He later said it was "the best book on investing ever written."

### Security Analysis (1934)

In 1934, Graham published "Security Analysis" with David Dodd. It laid out the principles of value investing. It is still used as a textbook.

## 4. THE GRIND — The Invisible Years (The Method)

Graham's method is defined by value, margin of safety, and Mr. Market.

### Value Investing

Graham's core principle: buy stocks below their intrinsic value. The intrinsic value is the true worth of the business. The market price fluctuates. The intelligent investor buys when the price is below value.

### Margin of Safety

Graham insisted on a margin of safety. Do not buy a stock if the price is close to value. Buy when the price is significantly below value. The margin protects you if you are wrong.

### Mr. Market

Graham introduced the "Mr. Market" metaphor. Imagine the market as a partner who offers you prices every day. Some days, Mr. Market is cheerful and offers high prices. Other days, he is despondent and offers low prices. You are free to accept or ignore his offers. The wise investor takes advantage of Mr. Market's moods.

### In the Short Run vs. the Long Run

Graham's famous quote: "In the short run, the market is a voting machine—but in the long run, it is a weighing machine." Prices reflect emotions in the short run. Prices reflect value in the long run.

The invisible years—the years after the 1929 crash—were years of learning. Graham lost money. He nearly went bankrupt. He vowed never to be that vulnerable again. He built a philosophy of margin of safety.

## 5. THE TEST — The Breakthrough Moment

The 1929 crash was the test. Graham lost money. He nearly went bankrupt. He rebuilt. He wrote "Security Analysis" and "The Intelligent Investor." He taught at Columbia. He created a legacy.

## 6. THE PHILOSOPHY — Stealable Wisdom

**On Value:**
"The intelligent investor is a realist who sells to optimists and buys from pessimists." Buy when others are selling. Sell when others are buying.

**On Margin of Safety:**
Always require a margin of safety. Never buy at fair value. Buy at a discount.

**On Mr. Market:**
The market is a partner who offers prices daily. You can accept or ignore. Do not let his moods control you.

**Key Quotes:**
- "In the short run, the market is a voting machine—but in the long run, it is a weighing machine."
- "The intelligent investor is a realist who sells to optimists and buys from pessimists."

## 7. THE LEGACY — Purpose Beyond Profit + Human Side

Benjamin Graham died in 1976. His books are still in print. His students still practice his principles.

### Warren Buffett

Graham's most famous student is Warren Buffett. Buffett studied under Graham at Columbia. He worked at Graham-Newman. He applied Graham's principles. He became the most successful investor of all time.

### Philanthropy

Graham was not known for philanthropy. He was known for teaching. He wrote books. He taught at Columbia.

### What does he regret?

Graham regretted the losses of 1929. He nearly went bankrupt. The experience shaped his philosophy of margin of safety.

### What does he want to be remembered for?

Graham wanted to be remembered for his principles. Value investing. Margin of safety. Mr. Market.
