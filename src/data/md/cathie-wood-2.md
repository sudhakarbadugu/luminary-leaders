# Cathie Wood

**Founder and CEO of ARK Invest**

## Early Life and Education

Catherine Wood was born in 1956 in Los Angeles, California. She studied finance and economics at the University of Southern California (USC), graduating with honors. Wood began her career as a research analyst at capital management firms, including Capital Group and T. Rowe Price. She developed a reputation for identifying disruptive innovation before mainstream Wall Street caught on. She spent decades studying the intersection of technology and capital markets.

## Career Before ARK Invest

Wood served as Chief Investment Officer at Valkyrie ETF Solutions and later worked at AllianceBernstein as a senior portfolio manager. At AllianceBernstein, she managed a $1.3 billion mutual fund focused on emerging technologies. Frustrated by the inability of traditional asset managers to invest freely in high-growth innovative companies, she decided to start her own firm. In 2014, she left AllianceBernstein and founded ARK Invest with backing from her own savings and a small group of initial investors.

## ARK Invest and Disruptive Innovation

ARK Invest became famous for its concentrated bets on what Wood called "genomic, robotics, AI, energy storage, and blockchain" companies. Wood led ARK to manage a series of exchange-traded funds (ETFs) focused on next-generation technologies. ARK's flagship fund, ARKK (ARK Innovation ETF), became one of the best-performing funds in the world from 2014 to 2020, returning over 500 percent and attracting over $50 billion in assets at its peak.

## The Tesla Bet

Wood's most famous call was on Tesla. She predicted Tesla would become a trillion-dollar company when most analysts had it below $100 billion. She bought Tesla shares at under $50 per split-adjusted price and held through the company's volatile growth. Her conviction was based on Tesla's data advantage from self-driving miles, battery technology, and vertical integration. Tesla became ARK's largest holding and the foundation of its performance story.

## The 2022 Crash and Redemption

In 2022, ARK's funds experienced catastrophic losses as the tech sector collapsed amid rising interest rates and risk-off sentiment. ARKK fell over 67 percent from its peak, and Wood faced intense criticism from media and institutional investors. She refused to sell her positions, insisting her models remained correct about long-term disruptive potential. She publicly acknowledged the painful drawdown but maintained her conviction that the companies she held were undervalued.

## Public Persona and Online Presence

Wood gained a cult following among retail investors, who referred to her as "Cathie Wood" with reverence. She gave frequent interviews, hosted podcasts, and published thick research reports explaining her thesis for each holding. She built a community of retail investors who defended her publicly against Wall Street criticism. She was known for her candid, optimistic style and a willingness to double down on contrarian positions under extreme pressure.

## Charitable and Educational Activities

Wood committed ARK to publishing its research publicly and free of charge, believing it democratized access to investment research. She spoke at universities and conferences about innovation-driven investing and the importance of supporting entrepreneurs. She served on the boards of various organizations and mentored young women in finance. She donated to USC and supported scholarships for students studying finance and technology.

## Net Worth and Recognition

Wood's net worth was estimated at several hundred million dollars, primarily from her stake in ARK Invest. She appeared on Forbes' lists of most powerful women and was named one of the world's 100 most influential people by Time magazine. She became a symbol of active, conviction-based investing in a world of passive index funds, and remained one of the most debated and followed investors of her generation.
