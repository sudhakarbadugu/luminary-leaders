# Usain Bolt — The Fastest Man Alive with 8 Olympic Golds

## 1. THE HOOK

He's the fastest man alive. 8 Olympic gold medals. World records in 100m, 200m, 4x100m. He says: "I don't think limits."

How does a boy from rural Jamaica become the fastest man in history?

## 2. ORIGIN — THE "Before" (The Relatability Engine)

Usain Bolt was born August 21, 1986, in Sherwood Content, Jamaica. He studied at William Knibb Memorial High School.

He was running from rural Jamaica. He saw sprinting. He wanted to be fastest.

## 3. THE FIRE — Failures, Rock-Bottom & Turning Points

### First Olympics (2004)

Bolt injured at Athens 2004. Eliminated in first round.

### Beijing 2008

Bolt won 3 gold medals. World records.

### London 2012

Bolt won 3 gold medals. "Triple-triple."

### Rio 2016

Bolt won 3 gold medals. 8 Olympic golds.

### World Records

Bolt holds world records: 100m (9.58s), 200m (19.19s), 4x100m relay.
