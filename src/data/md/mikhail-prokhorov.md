# Mikhail Prokhorov

## Early Life and Business Foundations

Mikhail Prokhorov was born on May 3, 1965, in Moscow, Soviet Union, into a family with strong technical and entrepreneurial backgrounds. His father was an engineer who managed a state-owned construction company, while his mother worked in economics, providing an environment that valued both technical skills and business acumen. Prokhorov studied at the Moscow State University of International Relations, preparing for a diplomatic career before the Soviet Union's dissolution redirected his ambitions toward business. He began his career in banking and commodity trading during Russia's chaotic post-Soviet transition period. His early business activities involved navigating the complexities and opportunities of Russian privatization, acquiring valuable assets during a period when many state enterprises became available to private investors.

## Rise in Russian Industry and Metals

Prokhorov built his initial fortune through his company ONEXIM Group, which he founded in the early 1990s. The conglomerate specialized in metals and mining investments, taking advantage of Russia's privatization era to accumulate significant industrial assets. At its peak, ONEXIM controlled Metalsider, Russia nickel producer Norilsk Nickel, and various other manufacturing and financial holdings. Prokhorov's business approach combined aggressive deal-making with careful attention to operational efficiency, creating value in industrial enterprises others considered inefficient. His wealth grew substantially during Russia's commodity boom years, establishing him among Russia's wealthiest individuals, commonly referred to as oligarchs in the international business press.

## Acquisition of the Brooklyn Nets

In 2010, Prokhorov purchased an 80% stake in the New Jersey Nets for approximately $200 million, with plans to relocate the franchise to Brooklyn and build a new arena. The acquisition represented a strategic pivot toward consumer-facing entertainment and sports team ownership. He invested heavily in transforming the Nets into a major market presence, including funding the construction of the Barclays Center arena in Brooklyn. Prokhorov took an active role in the team's operations, becoming known for his willingness to spend on player acquisition and marketing to make the Nets a championship contender. His ownership emphasized building the Nets as a global brand with international appeal, reflecting his cross-cultural business perspective.

## Political Career and Opposition

In 2011, Prokhorov announced his candidacy for the Russian presidency, challenging Prime Minister Vladimir Putin in elections scheduled for March 2012. His political platform advocated for modernization, anti-corruption measures, and greater economic freedom, positioning himself as a candidate for change. Prokhorov spent over $100 million on his campaign, traveling across Russia to build support among voters frustrated with corruption and stagnation. He was ultimately excluded from the ballot by electoral authorities on what widely seen as politically motivated technicalities, an outcome that generated international criticism of the Russian electoral system. The experience demonstrated the challenges facing independent political figures in Russia's managed democracy.

## Managing Sports Investments

## Technology Investment Activities

Prokhorov has invested significantly in technology companies and venture capital funds, leveraging his business expertise into the global technology sector. He has maintained interests in Russian technology companies and invested in various international startups through his investment vehicle, potentially including cryptocurrency and blockchain ventures. His technology investment philosophy emphasizes identifying companies with disruptive potential and providing not just capital but also strategic guidance drawn from his business experience. Prokhorov has shown interest in artificial intelligence, biotechnology, and other emerging technology sectors that align with his forward-looking business perspective. These investments represent diversification away from his traditional metals and industrial holdings.

## Philanthropy and Lifestyle

Prokhorov maintains an active philanthropy program focusing on education, culture, and supporting young entrepreneurs in Russia. He established foundations supporting Russian universities and cultural institutions, reflecting his belief in the importance of education and human capital development. His personal lifestyle includes interests in extreme sports including bungee jumping and base jumping, which have occasionally generated media attention and controversy. Prokhorov has been listed among the wealthiest individuals in Russia and globally, with estimated net worth varying based on asset valuations and market conditions.
