# Brad Feld — The Investor Who Said "Give First"

## 1. THE HOOK

In 2006, he cofounded Techstars. It became one of the world's largest startup accelerators. It funded 3,000+ companies. He is managing director at Foundry Group. He wrote "Startup Communities" and "Venture Deals." He coined "Give First"—help others without expecting anything in return. He blogs at Feld Thoughts since 2004. He says: "The best way to build a startup community is to give first."

How does an investor build a global startup movement on giving?

## 2. ORIGIN — THE "Before" (The Relatability Engine)

Brad Feld was born in 1965 or 1966. He studied at MIT. He became an entrepreneur and investor.

He was running from conventional finance. He saw the startup ecosystem. He understood community.

## 3. THE FIRE — Failures, Rock-Bottom & Turning Points

### Techstars (2006-Present)

In 2006, Feld cofounded Techstars with David Cohen, David Brown, and Jared Polis. It became one of the world's largest accelerators. It funded 3,000+ companies.

### Foundry Group

Feld is managing director at Foundry Group. The firm invests in early-stage technology companies.

### Books

Feld wrote "Startup Communities" (Boulder Thesis for building ecosystems), "Venture Deals" (with Jason Mendelson), and blogs at Feld Thoughts.

### Give First

Feld coined "Give First"—help others without expecting return. It became the Techstars philosophy.

## 4. THE GRIND — The Invisible Years (The Method)

Feld's method is defined by giving first, community building, and founder support.

### Give First

Feld's philosophy: "Give before you get." Help others. Build relationships. Returns come later.

### Community Building

Feld's Boulder Thesis: entrepreneurs are the lifeblood. Give first. Be inclusive. Build the ecosystem.

### Founder Mental Health

Feld speaks openly about mental health. He supports founders through struggles.

The invisible years—the years of blogging and building community—gave Feld influence. He shared freely.

## 5. THE TEST — The Breakthrough Moment

Techstars is the test. 3,000+ companies funded. The Give First philosophy scaled globally.

## 6. THE PHILOSOPHY — Stealable Wisdom

**On Giving:**
"Give before you get."

**On Community:**
"The best way to build a startup community is to give first."

**On Entrepreneurs:**
"Entrepreneurs are the lifeblood of a startup community."

## 7. THE LEGACY — Purpose Beyond Profit + Human Side

Brad Feld is still active. He invests. He writes. He supports founders.

### What does he want to be remembered for?

In interviews, Feld emphasizes giving and community. He wants investors to help founders.

Brad Feld's career demonstrates that giving first creates ecosystems.
