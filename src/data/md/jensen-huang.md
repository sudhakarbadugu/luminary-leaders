# Jensen Huang — NVIDIA CEO

## 1. THE HOOK
He built NVIDIA into the backbone of the AI revolution, with his GPUs powering everything from data centers to autonomous vehicles to ChatGPT. A former Denny's waiter who once dreamed of becoming a nuclear engineer, he transformed his company from a struggling graphics chip maker into the most valuable chip company in the world. His vision: "We're in the early stages of a computing revolution. The world will have a trillion dollars worth of AI infrastructure built in the next decade."

## 2. ORIGIN — THE "BEFORE" (The Relatability Engine)
Jensen Huang (Huang Jen-hsun) was born on February 17, 1963, in Taipei, Taiwan, to a family that immigrated to the United States when he was five years old. His father was an occupational safety engineer, and his mother was a schoolteacher. The family settled in Tacoma, Washington, where young Jensen attended high school and became a Eagle Scout. He developed an early fascination with electrical engineering, particularly reading about how engineers could control electrons—interest that would later translate into building microchips. He studied electrical engineering at Oregon State University, graduating in 1984, then earned his master's degree from Stanford in 1986. After Stanford, he worked at Advanced Micro Devices (AMD) and LSI Logic, gaining deep experience in chip architecture and design.

## 3. THE FIRE — Failures, Rock-Bottom & Turning Points
NVIDIA's early years were turbulent. Founded in 1993 with $40,000 of personal savings and a $5 million investment, NVIDIA nearly went bankrupt in 1996 when its first product—the NV1 chip—was a commercial failure. The company had to lay off half its workforce and pivot to a new strategy. The crisis moment came when the company was nearly delisted from NASDAQ in 1998 after failing to meet financial reporting requirements. A crucial partnership with Sega almost saved the company, but Sega's CEO canceled the deal over a disagreement about the chip's architecture. Jensen had to make an impossible phone call to Sega, asking them to honor the contract while admitting their chip wasn't good enough—a lesson in humility that shaped his management style. The pivot to standard graphics APIs and discrete GPUs eventually saved the company.

## 4. THE GRIND — The Invisible Years (The Method)
From 1986 to 1993, before founding NVIDIA, Huang worked at AMD and LSI Logic, mastering the semiconductor industry from both the product and business perspectives. At LSI, he worked on designing chips for hard drive controllers, learning how to build products that required extremely high reliability. He also developed the deep customer relationships that would later inform NVIDIA's approach—understanding that graphics wasn't just about technology but about delivering experiences gamers would pay for. In the early NVIDIA days, he personally visited game developers to understand what they needed, a hands-on approach that became institutionalized in NVIDIA's culture. He built the company's famous "founder's mentality"—treating every problem as an opportunity to learn and innovate.

## 5. THE TEST — THE BREAKTHROUGH MOMENT
NVIDIA's breakthrough came from an unexpected direction: cryptocurrency miners started buying gaming GPUs in 2017, creating a shortage that drove prices up and generated windfall profits. But the real transformation happened when AI researchers discovered that NVIDIA's gaming GPUs were perfect for training neural networks. The company's CUDA platform—developed years earlier to make programming GPUs easier—became the standard infrastructure for AI development. By 2023, NVIDIA's H100 chips were the most sought-after products in Silicon Valley, with data center revenue growing 400% in two years. This moment validated Huang's decade-long bet that GPU computing would eventually replace traditional CPU-based computing. The company's market cap exceeded $1 trillion, making it one of the five most valuable companies in the world.

## 6. THE PHILOSOPHY — Stealable Wisdom
**On Running:**
"The biggest mistake I've made is not learning from mistakes—it's running too fast and breaking things that shouldn't break."

**On Technology:**
"We build systems that last decades, not quarters. Our responsibility is to the future."

**On Competition:**
"Our competition is ourselves. We compete against our own ability to imagine what's next."

**On Crisis:**
"I've never found success by avoiding failure. I've found it by surviving it."

## 7. THE LEGACY — Purpose Beyond Profit + Human Side
NVIDIA under Huang has become the engine of modern AI, with every major AI system—OpenAI's GPT models, Google's Tensor Processing Units, Amazon's AI chips—running on or inspired by NVIDIA's architecture. Huang has committed the company to several ambitious goals: making AI accessible to every business, creating virtual reality tools for scientists and engineers, and building autonomous vehicles that could save millions of lives. He founded the NVIDIA Foundation and has donated to cancer research and STEM education programs. He married his high school sweetheart Lori Huang in 1991, and they have two children. Known for his distinctive leather jacket—worn as a trademark since the 1990s—Huang has become a symbol of visionary technology leadership, frequently delivering keynotes that shape industry expectations for years to come.
