# Adam Grant — The Youngest Tenured Professor at Wharton

## 1. THE HOOK

At 28, he became the youngest tenured professor at Wharton. He wrote "Give and Take." He proved that givers succeed more than takers in the long run. He wrote "Originals," "Think Again," "Hidden Potential." He hosts the WorkLife podcast. He says: "The most meaningful way to succeed is to help others succeed."

How does a psychologist from Detroit transform organizational behavior?

## 2. ORIGIN — THE "Before" (The Relatability Engine)

Adam Grant was born 1981 in Detroit, Michigan. He studied at Harvard (BA), University of Michigan (MA, PhD organizational psychology).

He was running from clinical work. He saw organizations. He wanted to understand workplace behavior.

## 3. THE FIRE — Failures, Rock-Bottom & Turning Points

### Wharton Professor

Grant became a professor at Wharton School, University of Pennsylvania. Youngest tenured professor at 28.

### Give and Take (2013)

In 2013, Grant wrote "Give and Take." Givers vs takers. Givers succeed in the long run.

### Originals (2016)

In 2016, Grant wrote "Originals." How non-conformists move the world.

### Option B (2017)

In 2017, Grant co-wrote "Option B" with Sheryl Sandberg. Resilience after adversity.

### Think Again (2021)

In 2021, Grant wrote "Think Again." Rethinking and unlearning.

### Hidden Potential (2023)

In 2023, Grant wrote "Hidden Potential." Untapped human capability.

### WorkLife Podcast

Grant hosts the WorkLife podcast. Workplace psychology.

## 4. THE GRIND — The Invisible Years (The Method)
