# Zinedine Zidane — The Headbutt and the 3 Champions League Titles

## 1. THE HOOK

He won the 1998 World Cup with two headers. He scored the greatest Champions League final volley. He headbutted Marco Materazzi in the 2006 World Cup final. He says: "I once cried because I had no shoes to play football with my friends. One day I met a man who had no feet."

How does a boy from Marseille's slums become a World Cup hero?

## 2. ORIGIN — THE "Before" (The Relatability Engine)

Zinedine Zidane was born June 23, 1972, in Marseille, France. Algerian immigrant parents. Grew up in La Castellane—one of Marseille's roughest neighborhoods. Discovered by scout at 14.

He was running from immigrant poverty. He saw football. He wanted elegance.

## 3. THE FIRE — Failures, Rock-Bottom & Turning Points

### 2002 World Cup

Zidane failed to deliver. France eliminated in group stage.

### 2006 World Cup Final Headbutt

Zidane headbutted Marco Materazzi. Sent off in extra time. France lost on penalties.

### Retirement

Zidane retired after 2006 World Cup. Controversial ending.

## 4. THE GRIND — The Invisible Years (The Method)

Zidane's method is defined by street football, silence, and technique.

### Street Football

Zidane's philosophy: La Castellane streets taught improvisation.

### Silence

Zidane's philosophy: "Silence is better than speaking badly."

### Technique

Zidane's philosophy: Practiced volleys for hours. 2002 CL volley wasn't luck.

## 5. THE TEST — THE BREAKTHROUGH MOMENT

1998 World Cup final is the test. Two headers. France's first World Cup.

## 7. THE LEGACY — Purpose Beyond Profit + Human Side

Zinedine Zidane managed Real Madrid. 3 consecutive Champions League titles. 1998 World Cup. 2000 Euro.

### What does he want to be remembered for?

In interviews, Zidane emphasizes perspective. He wanted to inspire immigrants.
