# Michael Phelps — The Most Decorated Olympian with 28 Medals

## 1. THE HOOK

He has 28 Olympic medals—most in history. 23 gold. He struggled with ADHD and depression. He says: "You can't put a limit on anything. The more you dream, the further you get."

How does a boy with ADHD become the most decorated Olympian?

## 2. ORIGIN — THE "Before" (The Relatability Engine)

Michael Phelps was born June 30, 1985, in Baltimore, Maryland. Studied at Towson University. Diagnosed with ADHD as a child.

He was running from ADHD. He saw swimming. He wanted to be great.

## 3. THE FIRE — Failures, Rock-Bottom & Turning Points

### First Olympics (2000)

Phelps competed at Sydney age 15. Youngest male Olympian since 1932.

### Athens 2004

Phelps won 6 gold medals. 8 medals total.

### Beijing 2008

Phelps won 8 gold medals. Most in single Olympics.

### London 2012

Phelps won 4 gold medals. Retired.

### Comeback (2016)

Phelps returned. Rio 2016. 5 gold medals. Retired again.

### Mental Health

Phelps struggled with depression. Sought therapy.

## 4. THE GRIND — The Invisible Years (The Method)

Phelps's method is defined by dreaming, consistency, and overcoming.

### Dream

Phelps's philosophy: "The more you dream, the further you get."

### Consistency

Phelps's philosophy: Swim every day. 365 days.

### Overcome

Phelps's philosophy: ADHD to greatness. Overcome.

## 5. THE TEST — THE BREAKTHROUGH MOMENT
