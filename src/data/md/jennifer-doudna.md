# Jennifer Doudna — The Nobel Laureate Who Edited the Code of Life

## 1. THE HOOK

In 2020, she won the Nobel Prize in Chemistry for CRISPR-Cas9 gene editing. With Emmanuelle Charpentier, she discovered "molecular scissors" that can edit DNA. She says: "We can now edit the code of life."

How does a biochemist from DC change the future of medicine?

## 2. ORIGIN — THE "Before" (The Relatability Engine)

Jennifer Doudna was born February 19, 1964, in Washington, D.C. She studied at Pomona College (BA chemistry), Harvard (PhD biological chemistry).

She was running from traditional chemistry. She saw RNA. She wanted to understand genetic code.

## 3. THE FIRE — Failures, Rock-Bottom & Turning Points

### UC Berkeley Professor (2002-Present)

Doudna became a professor at UC Berkeley. Howard Hughes Medical Institute investigator.

### CRISPR Research (2006-Present)

In 2006, Doudna began CRISPR research. Bacterial immune systems.

### CRISPR-Cas9 Discovery (2012)

In 2012, Doudna and Charpentier published CRISPR-Cas9 in Science. Gene editing breakthrough. "Molecular scissors." Edit DNA.

### Companies

Doudna cofounded Caribou Biosciences, Intellia Therapeutics, Mammoth Biosciences. CRISPR therapeutics.

### Nobel Prize (2020)

In 2020, Doudna won the Nobel Prize in Chemistry with Emmanuelle Charpentier. First all-female science Nobel team.

## 4. THE GRIND — The Invisible Years (The Method)

Doudna's method is defined by RNA research, collaboration, and ethical responsibility.

### RNA Research

Doudna's philosophy: Study bacterial immune systems. Applied science.

### Collaboration

Doudna's philosophy: Collaborate with Charpentier. Science is collaborative.

### Ethical Responsibility

Doudna's philosophy: "The ability to edit the genome is both a tremendous opportunity and a tremendous responsibility." Ethics matter.

The invisible years—the years of RNA research—gave Doudna the foundation. She discovered CRISPR.

## 5. THE TEST — THE BREAKTHROUGH MOMENT

Nobel Prize is the test. CRISPR-Cas9 gene editing. "Molecular scissors." We can edit the code of life.

## 7. THE LEGACY — Purpose Beyond Profit + Human Side

### What does she want to be remembered for?

In interviews, Doudna emphasizes ethical gene editing. She wants CRISPR to cure diseases responsibly.
