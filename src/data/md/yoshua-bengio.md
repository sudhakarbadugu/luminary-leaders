# Yoshua Bengio — The Pioneer Who Warns About AI

## 1. THE HOOK

In 2018, he won the Turing Award with Geoffrey Hinton and Yann LeCun. He is a professor at University of Montreal. He founded Mila—the Montreal AI Institute. He founded Element AI (acquired by ServiceNow). He pioneered deep learning theory, generative models, attention mechanisms. In recent years, he became an AI safety advocate. He founded an AI Safety Institute at Mila. He says: "We are at a point where AI systems are becoming powerful enough to be dangerous."

How does a pioneer become a protector?
