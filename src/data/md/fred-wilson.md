# Fred Wilson — The Blogger VC Who Invested in Twitter and Tumblr

## 1. THE HOOK

In 2003, he cofounded Union Square Ventures (USV). He invested in Twitter, Tumblr, Etsy, Zynga, Kickstarter, Stripe, Coinbase. He blogs daily at AVC.com since 2003. He coined "invest in large networks." He pioneered "mobile first" thesis. He says: "The best investments are the ones that are the most controversial." He is Fred Wilson.

How does a VC who blogs daily generate billions in returns?

## 2. ORIGIN — THE "Before" (The Relatability Engine)

Fred Wilson was born in 1961 or 1962. He worked at Euclid Partners before founding USV.

He was running from conventional finance. He saw the internet's potential. He understood networks.

## 3. THE FIRE — Failures, Rock-Bottom & Turning Points

### Euclid Partners

Wilson worked at Euclid Partners. He learned venture capital.

### Union Square Ventures (2003-Present)

In 2003, Wilson cofounded USV with Brad Burnham. The firm focuses on large networks.

### Investments

Wilson invested in Twitter (early investor, board member), Tumblr, Etsy, Zynga, Kickstarter, Stripe, Coinbase.

### AVC.com (2003-Present)

Since 2003, Wilson blogs daily at AVC.com. He shares thoughts on VC and startups.

## 4. THE GRIND — The Invisible Years (The Method)

Wilson's method is defined by thesis-driven investing, networks, and transparency.

### Invest in Large Networks

Wilson's philosophy: "We don't invest in companies, we invest in networks." He looks for businesses with network effects.

### Thesis-Driven

USV invests based on themes. Mobile first. Large networks. Applications on top of infrastructure.

### Transparency

Wilson blogs daily. He shares his thinking publicly. He builds trust through openness.

The invisible years—the years of blogging—were years of building reputation. Wilson shared his process. He attracted deal flow.

## 5. THE TEST — The Breakthrough Moment

The investments—Twitter, Tumblr, Stripe—are the test. Wilson identified network-effect businesses early.

## 6. THE PHILOSOPHY — Stealable Wisdom

**On Controversy:**
"The best investments are the ones that are the most controversial."

**On Networks:**
"We don't invest in companies, we invest in networks."

**On the Internet:**
"The internet is the most powerful tool we have for creating value."

## 7. THE LEGACY — Purpose Beyond Profit + Human Side

Fred Wilson is still active. He runs USV. He blogs.

### What does he want to be remembered for?

In interviews, Wilson emphasizes networks and transparency. He wants VCs to share their process.

## 8. FINAL MOTIVATION & PERSONAL APPLICATION Guide

Fred Wilson's career demonstrates that thesis-driven investing creates returns.

**5 Actionable Steps This Week:**

1. **Invest in networks** — "We don't invest in companies, we invest in networks." Look for network effects.

2. **Seek controversy** — "The best investments are the ones that are the most controversial." Go where others fear.

3. **Blog** — Wilson blogs daily. Share your thinking. Build reputation.

4. **Be thesis-driven** — Have an investment thesis. Let it guide decisions.

5. **Be transparent** — Share your process. Build trust.

**The Hard Question:**

If Wilson generates billions by investing in networks—are you looking for companies or networks?

Thesis-driven investing creates returns.
