# Mark Zuckerberg — The Dropout Who Connected the World

## 1. THE HOOK

At 19, he founded Facebook in his Harvard dorm room. He dropped out of Harvard. He acquired Instagram for $1B, WhatsApp for $19B, Oculus for $2B. He rebranded to Meta in 2021. Net worth $100B+. He says: "The biggest risk is not taking any risk."

How does a Harvard dropout connect 3 billion people?

## 2. ORIGIN — THE "Before" (The Relatability Engine)

Mark Zuckerberg was born May 14, 1984, in White Plains, New York. He studied at Harvard University (dropped out).

He was running from traditional education. He saw social networking. He wanted to connect people.

## 3. THE FIRE — Failures, Rock-Bottom & Turning Points

### Facebook (2004)

In 2004, Zuckerberg founded Facebook at Harvard. thefacebook.com. Harvard students only.

### Expansion (2006)

In 2006, Facebook opened to public. 3 billion+ users today.

### Instagram (2012)

In 2012, Zuckerberg acquired Instagram for $1B.

### WhatsApp (2014)

In 2014, Zuckerberg acquired WhatsApp for $19B.

### Oculus (2014)

In 2014, Zuckerberg acquired Oculus for $2B. VR.

### Privacy Controversies

Cambridge Analytica. Privacy scandals. Congressional hearings.

### Meta (2021)

In 2021, Zuckerberg rebranded Facebook to Meta. Metaverse vision.

## 4. THE GRIND — The Invisible Years (The Method)

Zuckerberg's method is defined by move fast, risk, and connecting the world.

### Move Fast and Break Things

Zuckerberg's philosophy: "Move fast and break things. Unless you are breaking stuff, you are not moving fast enough."

### Take Risks

Zuckerberg's philosophy: "The biggest risk is not taking any risk."

### Connect the World

Zuckerberg's philosophy: "By giving people the power to share, we're making the world more transparent."

The invisible years—the years of Harvard dorm—gave Zuckerberg the foundation. He connected 3 billion people.

## 5. THE TEST — THE BREAKTHROUGH MOMENT

3 billion users is the test. Instagram. WhatsApp. Connecting the world works.
