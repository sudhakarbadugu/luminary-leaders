# Kevin Systrom — The Cofounder Who Built Instagram in 8 Weeks and Sold It for $1B

## 1. THE HOOK

In 2010, he built Instagram in 8 weeks. In 2012, he sold it to Facebook for $1B. Only 13 employees at acquisition. 1 billion+ users under his leadership. He left Instagram in 2018 after tension with Zuckerberg. He says: "Every startup should address a real problem. It doesn't have to be huge, but it has to be real."

How does a Google engineer build a $1B photo app?

## 2. ORIGIN — THE "Before" (The Relatability Engine)

Kevin Systrom was born December 30, 1983, in Holliston, Massachusetts. He studied at Stanford University (BS management science & engineering 2006).

He was running from Gmail and Google Reader. He saw photo-sharing. He wanted to democratize photography.

## 3. THE FIRE — Failures, Rock-Bottom & Turning Points

### Google (2006-2009)

Systrom worked at Google. Gmail. Google Reader.

### Burbn (2010)

Systrom built Burbn. Location-based app. Feature-heavy. Unfocused.

### Instagram (2010-2018)

In 2010, Systrom and Mike Krieger stripped Burbn to photos. Named it Instagram. 25,000 users in first day.

### Facebook Acquisition (2012)

In 2012, Facebook acquired Instagram for $1B. 13 employees.

### 1 Billion Users

Under Systrom, Instagram grew to 1 billion+ users.

### Leave Instagram (2018)

In 2018, Systrom and Krieger left Instagram. Tension with Zuckerberg.

## 4. THE GRIND — The Invisible Years (The Method)

Systrom's method is defined by simplicity, filters, and real problems.

### Simplicity

Systrom's philosophy: "We focus on making the product really simple and making it really fast."

### Filters

Systrom's philosophy: Filters democratize photography. Everyone can make beautiful images.

### Real Problems

Systrom's philosophy: "Every startup should address a real problem. It doesn't have to be huge, but it has to be real."

The invisible years—the years at Google—gave Systrom product experience. He built Instagram.

## 5. THE TEST — THE BREAKTHROUGH MOMENT

$1B acquisition is the test. 1 billion users. Simplicity scales.

## 6. THE PHILOSOPHY — Stealable Wisdom

## 7. THE LEGACY — Purpose Beyond Profit + Human Side

## 8. FINAL MOTIVATION & PERSONAL APPLICATION Guide

Kevin Systrom's career demonstrates that simplicity scales.

**5 Actionable Steps This Week:**

1. **Solve real problems** — "Address a real problem." Don't chase trends.

2. **Strip features** — Instagram came from stripping Burbn. Remove, not add.

3. **Filters democratize** — Filters make everyone a photographer. Democratize.

4. **Small teams scale** — 13 employees at $1B acquisition. Lean teams.

5. **Leave on your terms** — Systrom left Instagram in 2018. Leave when values conflict.

**The Hard Question:**

If Systrom built a $1B app by stripping features—are you adding or removing?

Simplicity scales.
