# Brian Armstrong — The CEO Who Took Crypto Mainstream with Coinbase

## 1. THE HOOK

In 2012, he cofounded Coinbase. In 2021, he took it public—the first major crypto exchange IPO. He worked at Airbnb before. He believes crypto is the future of finance. He says: "I believe that digital currency is going to be the future of the financial system."

How does an Airbnb engineer build a $10B+ crypto exchange?

## 2. ORIGIN — THE "Before" (The Relatability Engine)

Brian Armstrong was born January 25, 1983, in San Jose, California. He studied at Rice University (BS computer science, BA economics 2005).

He was running from traditional finance. He saw crypto. He wanted to build an open financial system.
