# Li Ka-shing

**Chairman, Cheung Kong Holdings | Horizon Ventures | Net Worth: $30+ Billion**

Li Ka-shing is Hong Kong's most prominent businessman, building one of Asia's largest empires from humble beginnings. His investment strategy—focusing on infrastructure, utilities, and technology—has made him one of the most successful and respected entrepreneurs in Greater China and a model for generations of Asian business leaders.

## Early Life and Foundation of Business Empire

Li Ka-shing was born on June 13, 1928, in Chaozhou, Guangdong Province, China. His family fled to Hong Kong during the Japanese occupation of China in the 1940s. His father died of tuberculosis when Li was a teenager, forcing the young Li to leave school early and work to support his family. He started working at a plastics trading company and eventually became a plastics manufacturer himself. In 1950, he founded Cheung Kong Industries, starting with a small plastic manufacturing operation that would eventually grow into a diversified conglomerate.

## Building Cheung Kong Holdings

Li transformed his plastics company into a real estate and infrastructure empire through strategic acquisitions and disciplined patience. Cheung Kong Holdings became one of Hong Kong's largest companies, with interests in real estate development, telecommunications, ports, retail, and utilities. Li became known for his conservative financing approach—he famously avoided debt during his early years, preferring to build capital slowly rather than risk bankruptcy during economic downturns. This cautious approach would become his hallmark and the foundation of his long-term success.

## Investment Philosophy and Technology Bets

Li developed a reputation for identifying promising investments long before they became obvious to mainstream investors. He was an early investor in Facebook, placing $500 million in the social media giant in 2013, which generated enormous returns. Through his venture capital firm Horizon Ventures, Li invested in over 400 technology companies globally, including biotech, artificial intelligence, and renewable energy startups. His investment thesis focused on companies with transformative potential and sustainable competitive advantages—the same qualities he sought when acquiring businesses.

## philanthropy and Social Responsibility

Li established the Li Ka Shing Foundation in 1980, donating over $3 billion to charitable causes in Hong Kong, mainland China, and overseas. His foundation has funded universities, hospitals, and disaster relief efforts. Li pledged to donate at least one-third of his wealth to charity, influencing other wealthy Asians to follow his example. His approach to philanthropy emphasized measurable impact and sustainable development, funding educational and medical institutions that would serve communities for decades.

## Legacy and Succession

Li officially retired from his role as chairman of CK Hutchison Holdings in 2018, handing control to his eldest son, Victor Li. The transition was the culmination of decades of careful succession planning, ensuring that the empire would continue operating according to the principles that made Li successful. His younger son, Richard Li, manages other businesses independently. Li's reputation as Asia's most successful entrepreneur reflects not just his business achievements but his ethical standards and commitment to building value for society through commerce.
