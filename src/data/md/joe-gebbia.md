# Joe Gebbia — The Designer Who Co-founded Airbnb and Left to Explore

## 1. THE HOOK

In 2007, he couldn't afford rent. He rented air mattresses to conference attendees. In 2008, he cofounded Airbnb. In 2022, he left Airbnb. He followed his curiosity. He says: "Don't just follow your passion. Follow your curiosity."

How does a designer cofound a hospitality revolution?

## 2. ORIGIN — THE "Before" (The Relatability Engine)

Joe Gebbia was born 1981 in Atlanta, Georgia. He studied at Rhode Island School of Design (RISD, BFA graphic design 2005).

He was running from traditional design. He saw sharing economy. He wanted to create belonging.

## 3. THE FIRE — Failures, Rock-Bottom & Turning Points

### RISD Designer

Gebbia studied graphic design at RISD. Met Brian Chesky.

### San Francisco (2007)

In 2007, Gebbia moved to San Francisco. Couldn't afford rent.

### Airbed and Breakfast (2007)

Gebbia and Chesky rented air mattresses to conference attendees. Airbed and Breakfast.

### Airbnb (2008-2022)

In 2008, Gebbia cofounded Airbnb with Brian Chesky and Nathan Blecharczyk.

### Y Combinator (2009)

In 2009, Airbnb joined Y Combinator.

### IPO (2020)

In 2020, Airbnb IPO'd.

### Leave Airbnb (2022)

In 2022, Gebbia left Airbnb. Pursue new ventures.

## 4. THE GRIND — The Invisible Years (The Method)

Gebbia's method is defined by design, curiosity, and belonging.

### Design

Gebbia's philosophy: "Design is a way to solve problems." Apply design to everything.

### Curiosity

Gebbia's philosophy: "Don't just follow your passion. Follow your curiosity."

### Belonging

Gebbia's philosophy: "We wanted to create a world where you can belong anywhere."

The invisible years—the years after RISD—gave Gebbia design skills. He built Airbnb.
