# David Zaslav

**CEO of Warner Bros. Discovery**

## Early Life and Education

David Zaslav was born in 1960 in New York City, growing up in a working-class family in Queens. He attended the University of Pennsylvania's Wharton School of Business, where he graduated at the top of his class. After law school at Boston University, Zaslav worked at a prominent law firm before transitioning into media. He joined NBC's legal team in the early 1990s, which marked the beginning of his long career in the entertainment and media industry.

## Rise in Media Finance

Zaslav moved to the business side of media, serving as CFO and later president of NBC Cable, where he oversaw the company's portfolio of cable channels. He developed a reputation for financial discipline and an ability to negotiate complex deals. His understanding of both the creative and financial sides of the business made him uniquely valuable. He was credited with turning around underperforming cable properties and extracting value from existing assets.

## Leadership at Discovery

Zaslav became CEO of Discovery Communications in 2007, transforming the company into a global force in factual entertainment. Under his leadership, Discovery expanded internationally, launching channels in dozens of new markets. He acquired Scripps Networks Interactive in 2018 for $14.6 billion, adding Food Network, HGTV, and Travel Channel to the portfolio. The deal made Discovery one of the largest pay-television companies in the world. His tenure was marked by aggressive acquisitions and an instinct for identifying undervalued content assets.

## Merger with WarnerMedia

In 2022, Discovery merged with WarnerMedia (owned by AT&T) to form Warner Bros. Discovery. Zaslav became CEO of the combined entity, overseeing a portfolio that included Warner Bros. Studios, HBO, CNN, TBS, TNT, discovery+, and DC Comics. The merger was one of the largest media combinations in history, valued at over $43 billion. Zaslav faced the immediate challenge of integrating two very different corporate cultures and eliminating billions in costs to achieve synergies.

## Controversy and Cost-Cutting

Zaslav's leadership was marked by aggressive cost-cutting that drew criticism from the film industry. He oversaw the shutdown of CNN+ shortly after its launch, a decision that embarrassed the company and signaled a retreat from streaming innovation. He cut staff across divisions, reduced budgets for original programming, and removed content from HBO Max to save money on residuals and licensing fees. Filmmakers like Christopher Nolan and others publicly criticized his decisions as harmful to creative culture.

## Streaming Strategy and Financial Performance

Zaslav bet heavily on the combined company's streaming capabilities, merging HBO Max and Discovery+ into a single service. He argued that scale and content breadth would win in the streaming wars, competing with Netflix and Disney. Under his leadership, the company invested billions in content while simultaneously cutting costs. Wall Street analysts remained divided on whether his strategy would succeed given the intense competition and shifting consumer habits.

## Executive Compensation Controversy

Zaslav's compensation became a flashpoint for shareholder anger. In 2022, he earned $246 million in total compensation, making him one of the highest-paid CEOs in American corporate history. The figure drew intense criticism given widespread layoffs at the company, the cancellation of projects, and the company's declining stock price. Institutional shareholders pushed back, with advisory firms recommending votes against executive pay packages. Zaslav defended his compensation as aligned with long-term performance.

## Personal Style and Reputation

Zaslav was known as a shrewd negotiator who operated behind the scenes rather than seeking the spotlight. He rarely gave interviews or attended public events. Inside the company, he was respected for his financial acumen and criticized for his heavy-handed approach to creative decisions. He was known to read scripts and weigh in on content choices that affected the bottom line. Colleagues described him as intensely competitive, data-driven, and willing to make unpopular decisions when necessary.

## Net Worth and Future

Zaslav's net worth was estimated at over $400 million, primarily from his equity stake in Warner Bros. Discovery. His compensation package was tied to the company's performance over multi-year periods. He remained one of the most powerful and controversial figures in global media, responsible for steering one of the world's largest entertainment conglomerates through a period of unprecedented disruption in how people consumed video content.
