# Marie Curie — The Only Scientist to Win Two Nobel Prizes in Different Sciences

## 1. THE HOOK

She's the only person to win Nobel Prizes in two different sciences. Physics and Chemistry. She died of radiation exposure. She says: "Nothing in life is to be feared, it is only to be understood."

How does a woman in the 1900s win two Nobels?

## 2. ORIGIN — THE "Before" (The Relatability Engine)

Marie Curie was born November 7, 1867, in Warsaw, Poland. Moved to Paris. Studied at University of Paris (Sorbonne). First woman to win a Nobel Prize.

She was running from Poland's oppression. She saw science. She wanted discovery.

## 3. THE FIRE — Failures, Rock-Bottom & Turning Points

### Pierre's Death (1906)

Pierre Curie died in accident. Marie continued alone.

### Nobel Prize Physics (1903)

First woman to win Nobel Prize. Shared with Pierre.

### Nobel Prize Chemistry (1911)

Won second Nobel Prize. Alone.

### Death (1934)

Died of radiation exposure. Aplastic anemia.

## 4. THE GRIND — The Invisible Years (The Method)

Curie's method is defined by understanding, persistence, and sacrifice.

### Understanding

Curie's philosophy: "Nothing in life is to be feared, it is only to be understood."

### Persistence

Curie's philosophy: Isolate radium. Years of work.

### Sacrifice

Curie's philosophy: Died from radiation. Science over life.

## 5. THE TEST — THE BREAKTHROUGH MOMENT

Two Nobels in different sciences is the test. Understanding works.

## 7. THE LEGACY — Purpose Beyond Profit + Human Side
