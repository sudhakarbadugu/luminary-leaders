# Dario Amodei

**Cofounder and CEO of Anthropic**

## Early Life and Academic Background

Dario Amodei was born in 1984 in New York City to Italian immigrant parents. He grew up intellectually curious, studying physics and mathematics at the University of California, Berkeley, where he earned his PhD in physics. His academic work focused on machine learning and neural networks, placing him at the intersection of theoretical science and the emerging field of AI research. He worked alongside some of the founding researchers of modern deep learning during his time at Stanford and Berkeley.

## Work at OpenAI

Amodei joined OpenAI in its earliest days as one of the first research scientists. He rose to become the company's research director and later served as the VP of research. At OpenAI, he oversaw the development of groundbreaking models including GPT-2 and GPT-3, as well as the Codex system that powered GitHub Copilot. He became known for his rigorous approach to research and his ability to translate complex technical work into practical systems. He co-authored many of the company's most important papers and became a public voice on AI safety.

## Departure from OpenAI

In 2021, Amodei left OpenAI along with several other researchers, including his sister Daniela Amodei, who had served as the company's head of product. The departure followed disagreements about the company's direction and safety priorities. OpenAI had been moving faster toward commercial deployment, which some researchers felt was outpacing safety research. Amodei and his colleagues believed that building safe AI required a different organizational structure and a more conservative approach to deployment.

## Founding Anthropic

Amodei co-founded Anthropic in 2021 with the goal of building reliable, interpretable, and steerable AI systems. The company positioned itself as a "AI safety company," prioritizing research on alignment and robustness before releasing products. Anthropic raised over $7 billion from investors including Google, Spark Capital, and Salesforce CEO Marc Benioff. The company built its own large language model, Claude, as a direct competitor to GPT. Claude gained a reputation for being more cautious, constitutional, and safety-aligned than other models.

## Claude and the Claude Model Family

Anthropic released Claude in 2023 as a next-generation AI assistant. Claude was known for its nuanced responses, strong reasoning capabilities, and refusal to engage in harmful activities. The company released multiple versions, including Claude 2 and Claude 3, which surpassed GPT-4 on several benchmarks. Claude was deployed through business partnerships, including a major integration with Amazon Web Services and a consumer app available on iPhone and Android. The model gained a reputation for honesty and transparency about its own limitations.

## AI Safety Research Agenda

Amodei became one of the most prominent advocates for AI safety in the world. He wrote long-form essays on the risks of superintelligent AI, the challenges of alignment, and the need for interpretability research. He argued that the most capable AI systems could pose existential risks if built without sufficient safeguards. He pushed for international coordination on AI governance and supported regulation of frontier AI models. Anthropic published its "Constitutional AI" research, a framework for building AI systems that could self-critique and align with human values.

## Anthropic's Corporate Structure

Anthropic adopted a unique corporate structure designed to prioritize safety over profit. The company formed as a public benefit corporation, with a board that included independent safety experts. It allowed for a "capped profit" model for investors, limiting returns to multiples of their investment. This structure was designed to prevent the company from being captured by profit motives at the expense of safety. Amodei served as CEO, maintaining direct control over research direction and deployment decisions.

## Net Worth and Recognition

Amodei's net worth was estimated at over $18 billion in 2024, primarily from his stake in Anthropic, making him one of the wealthiest people in the AI field. Forbes listed him among the billionaires driving the AI revolution. He was named to Time magazine's list of the 100 most influential people in AI. He testified before Congress on AI safety and became a regular speaker at major tech conferences. He remained one of the most thoughtful and influential voices shaping the trajectory of the AI industry.
