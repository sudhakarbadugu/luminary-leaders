# Stephen Schwarzman — The King of Private Equity Who Built Blackstone

## 1. THE HOOK

In 1985, he cofounded Blackstone with $400,000. Today, Blackstone manages over $1 trillion. It is the world's largest alternative asset manager. It is the largest owner of real estate globally. He is called the "King of Private Equity." His net worth: $30-40 billion. He donated over $1 billion to charity. He founded Schwarzman Scholars—a $575 million program at Tsinghua University modeled on the Rhodes Scholarship. His famous quote: "In life, you don't get what you deserve, you get what you negotiate."

How does a man with $400,000 build a $1 trillion empire?

## 2. ORIGIN — THE "Before" (The Relatability Engine)

Stephen Schwarzman was born February 14, 1947, in Philadelphia, Pennsylvania. He studied at Yale and Harvard Business School. He started at Lehman Brothers. He became a managing director.

He was running from conventional careers. He could have stayed at Lehman Brothers. He chose to start his own firm.

## 3. THE FIRE — Failures, Rock-Bottom & Turning Points

### Lehman Brothers

Schwarzman started at Lehman Brothers. He became a managing director. He worked in investment banking.

### Founding Blackstone (1985)

In 1985, Schwarzman cofounded Blackstone with Pete Peterson. They started with $400,000. They built an empire.

### Blackstone Today

Blackstone manages over $1 trillion. It is the world's largest alternative asset manager. It is the largest owner of real estate globally.

### Private Equity

Blackstone pioneered "buy it, fix it, sell it." Acquire undervalued companies. Improve operations. Sell at a premium.

### Real Estate

Blackstone became the largest real estate investor globally. It owns property portfolios across the world.

### Dealmaking
