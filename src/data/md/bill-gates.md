# Bill Gates — The Philanthropist Who Put a Computer on Every Desk

## 1. THE HOOK

In 1975, he cofounded Microsoft with Paul Allen. He dropped out of Harvard. He became the world's richest person. He pledged to give away 99% of his wealth. He says: "A computer on every desk and in every home."

How does a Harvard dropout become the world's biggest philanthropist?

## 2. ORIGIN — THE "Before" (The Relatability Engine)

Bill Gates was born October 28, 1955, in Seattle, Washington. He studied at Harvard (dropped out).

He was running from traditional education. He saw PCs. He wanted software on every desk.

## 3. THE FIRE — Failures, Rock-Bottom & Turning Points

### Microsoft (1975-2000)

In 1975, Gates cofounded Microsoft with Paul Allen. He became CEO. Microsoft became the world's largest PC software company.

### Windows (1985)

In 1985, Microsoft launched Windows. GUI operating system. Dominance.

### Chairman (2000-2014)

In 2000, Gates stepped down as CEO. He became chairman. He remained largest shareholder until 2014.

### Bill & Melinda Gates Foundation (2000-Present)

In 2000, Gates founded Bill & Melinda Gates Foundation. Global health, vaccines, education, climate. Billions given.

### Giving Pledge

Gates and Warren Buffett created the Giving Pledge. Pledge to give away 99% of wealth.

### Philanthropy Focus (2008-Present)

In 2008, Gates transitioned to full-time philanthropy. Gates Foundation is his full-time work.

## 4. THE GRIND — The Invisible Years (The Method)

Gates' method is defined by PC revolution, philanthropy, and giving back.

### PC Revolution

Gates' philosophy: "A computer on every desk and in every home." Software for all.

### Philanthropy

Gates' philosophy: "I have been given the blessings of wealth, and with that comes the responsibility to give back." Global health. Vaccines.

### Success

Gates' philosophy: "Success is a lousy teacher. It seduces smart people into thinking they can't lose." Stay humble.

The invisible years—the years of Microsoft—gave Gates wealth. He gave it away.

## 5. THE TEST — THE BREAKTHROUGH MOMENT

World's richest person is the test. Windows dominance. Giving Pledge. Philanthropy scales.

## 6. THE PHILOSOPHY — Stealable Wisdom

**On Success:**
"Success is a lousy teacher. It seduces smart people into thinking they can't lose."

**On Responsibility:**
"I have been given the blessings of wealth, and with that comes the responsibility to give back."

## 7. THE LEGACY — Purpose Beyond Profit + Human Side

Bill Gates runs the Gates Foundation. He advocates for climate change. He writes annual letters.

### What does he want to be remembered for?

In interviews, Gates emphasizes philanthropy and global health. He wants to eradicate polio.

Bill Gates' career demonstrates that wealth enables philanthropy.
