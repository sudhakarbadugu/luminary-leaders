# Peter Fenton — The Low-Profile VC Who Invested in Twitter and Yelp

## 1. THE HOOK

He is a partner at Benchmark Capital. He was an early investor in Twitter, Yelp, New Relic, Zuora, Elastic, and Cockroach Labs. He joined Benchmark in 2006. He is known for low ego, founder focus, and long-term orientation. He rarely gives interviews. He is considered one of the most thoughtful VCs. His philosophy: "invest in only the best founders." He helps founders without being in the way. He is Peter Fenton.

How does a low-profile VC generate multiple $1 billion exits?

Peter Fenton was born in 1972 or 1973. He studied at Stanford (BA) and Stanford (MBA). He worked at Accel Partners before Benchmark.

He was running from conventional finance. He saw the opportunity in venture capital. He joined Accel.

## 4. THE GRIND — The Invisible Years (The Method)

Fenton's method is defined by founder focus, concentrated portfolio, and network.

### Invest in the Best Founders

Fenton's philosophy: "invest in only the best founders." He does not invest in ideas. He invests in people.

### Help Without Being in the Way

Fenton's philosophy: "I like to be helpful without being in the way." He supports founders. He does not dictate.

### Concentrated Portfolio

Benchmark takes a concentrated approach. They invest in few companies. They go deep.

### Network-Driven Deal Flow

Fenton's deals come from his network. He builds relationships. He does not chase momentum.

The invisible years—the years of building his network—were years of relationship building. Fenton did not seek publicity. He did the work.
