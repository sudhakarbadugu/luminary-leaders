# Charlie Munger — The Inverting Sage Who Partnered with Buffett for 45 Years

## 1. THE HOOK

For 45 years, he was Warren Buffett's indispensable partner. He turned "avoiding stupidity" into a philosophy. He said: "Invert, always invert." He died at 99 in 2023. He built a $2B+ fortune. He says: "Tell me where I'm going to die so I never go there."

How does a lawyer build a latticework of mental models?

## 2. ORIGIN — THE "Before" (The Relatability Engine)

Charlie Munger was born January 1, 1924, in Omaha, Nebraska. He studied at University of Michigan (dropped out), University of Michigan Law School (JD 1948).

He was running from traditional law. He saw investing. He wanted to think rationally.

## 3. THE FIRE — Failures, Rock-Bottom & Turning Points

### Law Career

Munger built a law firm—Munger Tolles & Olson. Successful.

### Personal Tragedy (1950s)

Divorce. Son Teddy died of leukemia. Lost everything.

### Berkshire Hathaway (1978-2023)

In 1978, Munger joined Berkshire Hathaway as vice chairman. Partnered with Warren Buffett for 45 years.

### Blindness (2000s)

Munger lost sight in one eye. Adapted. Continued working.

### Death (2023)

Munger died November 28, 2023, at 99.

## 4. THE GRIND — The Invisible Years (The Method)

Munger's method is defined by inversion, mental models, and rationality.

### Inversion

Munger's philosophy: "Invert, always invert." Avoid stupidity. Ask what causes failure.

### Mental Models

Munger's philosophy: "Latticework of mental models." Learn from multiple disciplines.

### Rationality

Munger's philosophy: "I never allow myself to have an opinion on anything that I don't know the other side's argument better than they do."

The invisible years—the years of law—gave Munger discipline. He built mental models.

## 5. THE TEST — THE BREAKTHROUGH MOMENT

45-year partnership is the test. Berkshire Hathaway. Inversion works.

## 6. THE PHILOSOPHY — Stealable Wisdom

**On Inversion:**
"Invert, always invert."

**On Dying:**

**On Patience:**

**On Opinions:**

## 7. THE LEGACY — Purpose Beyond Profit + Human Side

Charlie Munger died in 2023. He left Berkshire Hathaway. He left mental models.

### What does he want to be remembered for?

In interviews, Munger emphasized rationality and inversion. He wanted to avoid stupidity.

## 8. FINAL MOTIVATION & PERSONAL APPLICATION Guide

Charlie Munger's career demonstrates that inversion avoids failure.
