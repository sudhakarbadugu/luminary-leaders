# Joe Biden

## Early Life and Political Awakening

Joseph Robinette Biden Jr. was born on November 20, 1942, in Scranton, Pennsylvania, the first of four children in a working-class family. His father, James Biden, experienced financial difficulties that forced the family to relocate to modest surroundings during Biden's childhood. These early experiences with economic hardship shaped Biden's worldview and his deep identification with ordinary Americans facing financial struggles. He would often reference his father's mantra about the importance of dignity in work and the need to protect working families.

## Tragedy and Resilience

In 1972, shortly after his election to the U.S. Senate at age 29, Biden's wife Neilia and infant daughter Naomi were killed in an automobile accident while Christmas shopping. His sons Beau and Hunter survived but were seriously injured. Rather than abandoning his political career, Biden was sworn in as senator from his hospital room while his sons recovered. This devastating loss became a defining moment that forged his resilience and deep empathy for others experiencing grief. He commuted daily from Wilmington, Delaware, to Washington D.C. to remain close to his sons during their recovery.

## Senate Career and Rise to Prominence

Biden served as a U.S. Senator from Delaware from 1973 to 2009, building a reputation for foreign policy expertise and bipartisan deal-making. He chaired the Senate Judiciary Committee and was instrumental in crafting the 1994 Crime Bill and the Violence Against Women Act. His tenure included 36 years of service, during which he developed relationships across the aisle and became known for his ability to work with political opponents. Biden ran for president in 1988 and 2008 before finally succeeding in his third campaign.

## Vice Presidency and Obama Years

Biden served as Vice President under Barack Obama from 2009 to 2017, becoming the longest-serving Vice President in American history. He played key roles in managing the 2010 economic recovery, the assassination of Osama bin Laden, and relations with foreign leaders. Obama described Biden as a "brother" and valued his counsel on matters ranging from legislative strategy to personal matters. The relationship between the two leaders became one of the closest political partnerships in modern American history.

## 2020 Presidential Campaign

Biden entered the 2020 Democratic primary following the death of his son Beau from cancer in 2015, framing his campaign around restoring the soul of America. He positioned himself as a return to normalcy following Donald Trump's turbulent first term. Despite early primary setbacks, Biden's broad coalition of moderates, progressives, and suburban voters propelled him to victory. The campaign coincided with nationwide protests following George Floyd's murder, and Biden centered his message on unity and healing national divisions.

## 46th President of the United States

Biden assumed the presidency in January 2021 amid the COVID-19 pandemic and economic crisis. His first hundred days saw the passage of a $1.9 trillion American Rescue Plan and the initiation of widespread COVID vaccination. His administration managed the U.S. withdrawal from Afghanistan and navigated the Russia-Ukraine conflict by supplying military aid to Kyiv. Biden also passed major legislation including the CHIPS Act, the Infrastructure Investment Act, and the Inflation Reduction Act. His legislative achievements represented substantial domestic investment in climate, technology, and social programs.

## Challenges and Legacy

Biden's presidency faced record inflation, border controversies, and questions about his age and cognitive sharpness. His son Hunter Biden's legal troubles became a political liability, while Biden's decision to remain in the race despite low approval ratings drew criticism. A disastrous debate performance in June 2024 led to his withdrawal from the campaign, with Vice President Kamala Harris becoming the Democratic nominee. Biden completed his term with mixed results—stabilized economy but persistent political polarization and questions about democratic norms.

## Personal Life and Character

Biden has maintained an extraordinary five-decade political career marked by personal tragedy, comeback resilience, and consistent core values. Known for his verbose speaking style and physical proximity to voters, he cultivated an image as a man of the people despite decades in Washington. His faith, family loyalty, and personal warmth have been constants throughout his career. While his legacy remains contested, Biden's influence on American politics spanning nearly half a century is undeniable, with his longevity and persistence unmatched in modern political history.
