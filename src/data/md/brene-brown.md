# Brené Brown — The Researcher Who Made Vulnerability a Strength

## 1. THE HOOK

In 2010, she gave a TEDx talk called "The Power of Vulnerability." It went viral. 60M+ views. One of the most-watched TED talks ever. She spent decades researching shame and vulnerability. She wrote 5 #1 New York Times bestsellers. She says: "Vulnerability is the birthplace of innovation, creativity, and change."

How does a social work researcher become a global leadership voice?

## 2. ORIGIN — THE "Before" (The Relatability Engine)

Brené Brown was born 1965 in San Antonio, Texas. She studied at University of Houston (BSW, MSW, PhD social work).

She was running from comfort. She saw shame and vulnerability. She wanted to understand human connection.

## 3. THE FIRE — Failures, Rock-Bottom & Turning Points

### Research on Shame and Vulnerability

Brown spent decades researching shame, vulnerability, courage, empathy, and connection. Qualitative research. Grounded theory.

### TEDxHouston 2010

In 2010, Brown gave a TEDx talk: "The Power of Vulnerability." It went viral. 60M+ views. Global recognition.

### TED 2012

In 2012, Brown gave a TED talk: "Listening to Shame." She explored shame and how to overcome it.

### Daring Greatly (2012)

In 2012, Brown wrote "Daring Greatly." Embracing vulnerability. Living wholeheartedly.

### Rising Strong (2015)

In 2015, Brown wrote "Rising Strong." Rebounding from failure.

### Braving the Wilderness (2017)

In 2017, Brown wrote "Braving the Wilderness." Belonging, courage, standing alone.

### Dare to Lead (2018)

In 2018, Brown wrote "Dare to Lead." Leadership through vulnerability and trust.

### Atlas of the Heart (2021)

In 2021, Brown wrote "Atlas of the Heart." Mapping human emotions.

### Unlocking Us Podcast

Brown hosts "Unlocking Us" podcast. Conversations on vulnerability, courage, leadership.

### Dare to Lead Certification

Brown created Dare to Lead Facilitator Certification. Training for leaders.

## 4. THE GRIND — The Invisible Years (The Method)

Brown's method is defined by research, vulnerability, and courage frameworks.

### Research-Based Frameworks

Brown's philosophy: Grounded theory research. Qualitative. Data-driven.

### Vulnerability as Strength

Brown's philosophy: Vulnerability is not weakness. It's the foundation of courage, connection, leadership.

### Shame Resilience

Brown's philosophy: Build shame resilience. Acknowledge shame. Move through it.

The invisible years—the years of research—gave Brown credibility. She became a global voice.

## 5. THE TEST — THE BREAKTHROUGH MOMENT

60M+ TED views is the test. 5 #1 bestsellers. Vulnerability as strength works.

## 6. THE PHILOSOPHY — Stealable Wisdom

**On Vulnerability:**
"Vulnerability is the birthplace of innovation, creativity, and change."

**On Courage:**
"Courage is a heart word. The root of the word courage is cor—the Latin word for heart."

**On Emotions:**
"We cannot selectively numb emotions. When we numb the dark, we numb the light."

## 7. THE LEGACY — Purpose Beyond Profit + Human Side

Brené Brown is still researching, writing, podcasting. She trains leaders.

### What does she want to be remembered for?

In interviews, Brown emphasizes vulnerability and courage. She wants people to live wholeheartedly.
