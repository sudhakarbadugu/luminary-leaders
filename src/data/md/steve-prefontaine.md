# Steve Prefontaine — The Runner Who Gave Everything and Inspired Generations

## 1. THE HOOK

He set American records at every distance from 2,000m to 10,000m. He was 24. He died in a car accident. He was Nike's first sponsored athlete. He says: "To give anything less than your best is to sacrifice the gift."

How does a runner from Coos Bay become a legend at 24?

## 2. ORIGIN — THE "Before" (The Relatability Engine)

Steve Prefontaine was born January 25, 1951, in Coos Bay, Oregon. He studied at University of Oregon.

He was running from small-town life. He saw running. He wanted to be the best.

## 3. THE FIRE — Failures, Rock-Bottom & Turning Points

### University of Oregon

Prefontaine ran at Oregon. Coached by Bill Bowerman. 7 NCAA championships.

### American Records

Prefontaine set American records at every distance from 2,000m to 10,000m.

### Munich Olympics (1972)

In 1972, Prefontaine competed in Munich Olympics. 4th in 5000m.

### Nike's First Sponsored Athlete

Prefontaine was Nike's first sponsored athlete.

### Death (1975)

On May 30, 1975, Prefontaine died in a car accident. Age 24.

## 4. THE GRIND — The Invisible Years (The Method)

Prefontaine's method is defined by giving best, bleeding, and running.

### Give Your Best

Prefontaine's philosophy: "To give anything less than your best is to sacrifice the gift."

### Bleed

Prefontaine's philosophy: "Somebody may beat me, but they're going to have to bleed to do it."

### Runner

Prefontaine's philosophy: "I'm not a jogger, I'm a runner."

The invisible years—the years at Oregon—gave Prefontaine training. He became a legend.

## 5. THE TEST — THE BREAKTHROUGH MOMENT

American records is the test. Munich Olympics. Inspiration works.
