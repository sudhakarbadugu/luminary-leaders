# Simone Biles — The Most Decorated Gymnast Who Put Mental Health First

## 1. THE HOOK

She has 37 World Championship medals—most in history. 7 Olympic medals. She withdrew from Tokyo 2020 to protect her mental health. She won gold at Paris 2024. She says: "Put mental health first."

How does a girl from foster care become the greatest gymnast?

## 2. ORIGIN — THE "Before" (The Relatability Engine)

Simone Biles was born March 14, 1997, in Columbus, Ohio. Raised by grandparents in Houston after foster care. Discovered gymnastics at age 6.

She was running from foster care. She saw gymnastics. She wanted to fly.

## 3. THE FIRE — Failures, Rock-Bottom & Turning Points

### First World Title (2013)

Biles became first African-American to win world all-around title.

### Rio Olympics (2016)

Biles won 4 gold medals. Dominant.

### The Twisties (Tokyo 2020)

Biles suffered "twisties"—dangerous disconnect between mind and body. Withdrew from competition.

### Comeback (Paris 2024)

Biles returned. Won team gold, all-around gold, vault silver.

## 4. THE GRIND — The Invisible Years (The Method)

Biles's method is defined by pushing limits and mental health.

### Push Limits

Biles's philosophy: Invented skills named after her. Biles, Biles II, Yurchenko double pike.

### Mental Health

Biles's philosophy: "Put mental health first." Protect yourself.

### Authenticity

Biles's philosophy: "I'm not the next Usain Bolt or Michael Phelps. I'm the first Simone Biles."
