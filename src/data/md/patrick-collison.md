# Patrick Collison — The Fast Learner Who Built Internet Infrastructure

## 1. THE HOOK

In 2010, he cofounded Stripe with his brother John. They were 22 and 20. They dropped out of MIT and Harvard. They went through Y Combinator in 2011. At 31, Patrick became the world's youngest self-made billionaire. Stripe reached $95 billion at peak. He says: "Most people overestimate what they can do in a year and underestimate what they can do in ten years."

How did a boy from rural Ireland build payment infrastructure for the internet?

## 2. ORIGIN — THE "Before" (The Relatability Engine)

Patrick Collison was born September 9, 1988, in Dromineer, County Tipperary, Ireland. He grew up in a small village on Lough Derg. He studied at University of Limerick, then MIT (dropped out).

He was running from rural Ireland. He saw payment infrastructure. He wanted to solve the "accept payments" problem.

## 3. THE FIRE — Failures, Rock-Bottom & Turning Points

### Early Entrepreneurship

Patrick founded Shuppa with John. An auction management platform. Later merged with Auctomatic.

### Stripe (2010-Present)

In 2010, Patrick and John founded Stripe. They wanted to make accepting payments online simpler. Y Combinator 2011.

### Stripe's Growth

Stripe expanded to 50+ countries. Stripe Atlas for startup incorporation. Stripe Press book publisher. Stripe Climate for carbon removal.

### Valuation

Stripe valued at $95 billion at peak. Now ~$50 billion.

### Youngest Self-Made Billionaire

At 31, Patrick became the world's youngest self-made billionaire. (John was 27.)

## 4. THE GRIND — The Invisible Years (The Method)

Patrick's method is defined by fast learning, long-term thinking, and curiosity.

### Fast Learning

Patrick's reputation: reads widely, synthesizes quickly. Writes collison.org blog. Interviews thinkers.

### Long-Term Thinking

Patrick's philosophy: "Most people overestimate what they can do in a year and underestimate what they can do in ten years."

### The Collison Brothers

Patrick and John are known as the "Collison brothers." Patrick is CEO. John is President. Successful sibling collaboration.

The invisible years—the years of Shuppa and Auctomatic—taught Patrick entrepreneurship. He built Stripe.

## 5. THE TEST — THE BREAKTHROUGH MOMENT

$95 billion valuation is the test. Stripe became payment infrastructure for the internet. Infrastructure works.
