# Eduardo Saverin — The Facebook Cofounder Who Sued Zuckerberg and Won

## 1. THE HOOK

In 2004, he cofounded Facebook with Mark Zuckerberg. He was the business manager and CFO. In 2005, Zuckerberg diluted his shares. He sued. In 2009, he settled. In 2012, he renounced US citizenship. He moved to Singapore. He says: "I don't regret the things I've done. I regret the things I didn't do."

How does a cofounder get dilute...d and come back?

## 2. ORIGIN — THE "Before" (The Relatability Engine)

Eduardo Saverin was born March 19, 1982, in São Paulo, Brazil. He studied at Harvard University (graduated 2006).

He was running from family business. He saw social networking. He wanted to invest.

## 3. THE FIRE — Failures, Rock-Bottom & Turning Points

### Facebook (2004-2005)

In 2004, Saverin cofounded Facebook. Business manager and CFO.

### Share Dilution (2005)

In 2005, Zuckerberg diluted Saverin's shares. From 30% to less than 10%.

### Lawsuit (2005-2009)

Saverin sued Zuckerberg. Share dilution. Settled in 2009.

### Singapore (2009-Present)

In 2009, Saverin moved to Singapore. Investor in Asian tech startups.

### Renounce US Citizenship (2012)

In 2012, Saverin renounced US citizenship. Tax reasons.

### B Capital

Saverin cofounded B Capital. Venture fund.

### Asia Focus
