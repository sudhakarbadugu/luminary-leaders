# Dara Khosrowshahi — The Diplomat Who Fixed Uber's Culture

## 1. THE HOOK

In 2017, he was hired to fix Uber. He succeeded Travis Kalanick—the controversial founder. Uber had "bro culture" scandals. He transformed the culture. He took Uber public in 2019. He navigated COVID. He focuses on profitability. He says: "The best companies are built on trust."

How does an Iranian immigrant rescue a scandal-plagued company?

## 2. ORIGIN — THE "Before" (The Relatability Engine)

Dara Khosrowshahi was born May 28, 1969, in Tehran, Iran. His family fled the Iranian Revolution in 1978. He studied at Brown University (BS electrical engineering).

He was running from revolution. He saw opportunity in America. He wanted to build businesses.

## 3. THE FIRE — Failures, Rock-Bottom & Turning Points

### Allen & Company

Khosrowshahi started as an investment banker at Allen & Company. Finance. Corporate strategy.

### IAC and Expedia (2005-2017)

Khosrowshahi joined IAC. In 2005, he became CEO of Expedia Group. He grew it into a travel giant. 12 years. Reputation for steady leadership.

### Uber CEO (2017-Present)

In August 2017, Khosrowshahi was recruited to lead Uber. He succeeded Travis Kalanick. Uber faced sexual harassment allegations, toxic culture, regulatory battles, investor revolt.

### Cultural Transformation

Khosrowshahi replaced "growth at all costs" with trust and accountability. He implemented new HR policies. He hired diverse leadership. He improved driver relationships.

### Uber IPO (2019)

In 2019, Khosrowshahi took Uber public. One of the largest tech IPOs in history.

### COVID (2020)

In 2020, COVID devastated ride-sharing. Khosrowshahi navigated the crisis.

### Profitability Focus

Khosrowshahi steered Uber toward profitability. Diversified services: Uber Eats, Uber Freight.
