# Brian Halligan — The Inbound Marketing Pioneer Who Built HubSpot

## 1. THE HOOK

In 2006, he cofounded HubSpot with Dharmesh Shah. They popularized "inbound marketing." Instead of interrupting customers, attract them. HubSpot grew to $1B+ revenue. He stepped down as CEO in 2021. He is now executive chairman. He says: "The best way to grow a business is to stop interrupting people."

How does a sales executive disrupt marketing?

## 2. ORIGIN — THE "Before" (The Relatability Engine)

Brian Halligan studied at University of New Hampshire (BS), MIT (MBA). He worked at PTC as a sales executive. He worked at Groove Networks (acquired by Microsoft). He was a venture partner at General Catalyst.

He was running from traditional marketing. He saw interruption was broken. He wanted to attract customers.

## 3. THE FIRE — Failures, Rock-Bottom & Turning Points

### Sales Executive

Halligan worked at PTC. Sales. He worked at Groove Networks. Acquired by Microsoft.

### General Catalyst

Halligan was a venture partner at General Catalyst. He saw startups struggle with marketing.

### HubSpot (2006-Present)

In 2006, Halligan cofounded HubSpot with Dharmesh Shah. Inbound marketing platform. Attract customers, not interrupt them.

### Inbound Marketing Philosophy

Halligan popularized "inbound marketing." The term transformed marketing. Instead of cold calls, ads, spam—create content that attracts.

### HubSpot Growth

HubSpot grew to $1B+ revenue. Millions of customers. Inbound marketing became the standard.

### HubSpot IPO (2014)

In 2014, Halligan took HubSpot public.

### Step Down (2021)

In 2021, Halligan stepped down as CEO. He became executive chairman. Yamini Rangan became CEO.

Halligan's method is defined by inbound marketing, culture, and market disruption.
