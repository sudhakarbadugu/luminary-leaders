# Bill Bowerman — The Coach Who Cofounded Nike and Invented Running Shoes

## 1. THE HOOK

He coached 31 Olympians. He won 4 NCAA championships. In 1964, he cofounded Nike with Phil Knight. He poured rubber into a waffle iron—the Waffle Trainer. He wrote "Jogging"—popularized jogging in America. He says: "If you have a body, you are an athlete."

How does a track coach revolutionize footwear?

## 2. ORIGIN — THE "Before" (The Relatability Engine)

Bill Bowerman was born February 19, 1911, in Portland, Oregon. He studied at University of Oregon (BS 1934).

He was running from traditional athletics. He saw running shoes. He wanted to make athletes faster.

## 3. THE FIRE — Failures, Rock-Bottom & Turning Points

### High School Coach

Bowerman coached high school track.

### University of Oregon (1948-1972)

In 1948, Bowerman became head track coach at University of Oregon. 4 NCAA championships. 31 Olympians.

### Steve Prefontaine

Bowerman coached Steve Prefontaine. Running legend.

### Blue Ribbon Sports (1964)

In 1964, Bowerman cofounded Blue Ribbon Sports with Phil Knight. $500 each.

### Waffle Trainer (1971)

Bowerman poured rubber into a waffle iron. The Waffle Trainer. Revolution.

### Jogging Book

Bowerman wrote "Jogging." Popularized jogging in America.
