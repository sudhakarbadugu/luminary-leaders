# Katalin Karikó — The Scientist Who Persisted for 40 Years to Save Millions

## 1. THE HOOK

In 2023, she won the Nobel Prize in Physiology or Medicine for mRNA vaccines. For 40 years, she researched mRNA when everyone said it was impossible. She was demoted at Penn. She was nearly fired multiple times. Her mRNA research enabled COVID-19 vaccines. She says: "I just wanted to make it work."

How does a demoted scientist save millions of lives?

## 2. ORIGIN — THE "Before" (The Relatability Engine)

Katalin Karikó was born January 17, 1955, in Szolnok, Hungary. She studied at University of Szeged (PhD biochemistry).

She was running from Hungarian communism. She saw mRNA. She wanted to make it work.

## 3. THE FIRE — Failures, Rock-Bottom & Turning Points

### University of Szeged

Karikó studied biochemistry in Hungary. She left for the US.

### Temple University, University of Pennsylvania

Karikó moved to Temple University, then University of Pennsylvania. Adjunct professor. Demoted. Nearly fired multiple times.

### mRNA Research (1980s-2020s)

Karikó researched mRNA since the 1980s. Funding rejected again and again. Everyone said mRNA would never work.

### mRNA Breakthrough (2005)

In 2005, Karikó and Drew Weissman published mRNA modification in Immunity. Modified mRNA avoids immune rejection. Breakthrough.

### BioNTech (2013-2022)

In 2013, Karikó joined BioNTech as senior vice president. She kept researching mRNA.

### COVID-19 mRNA Vaccines (2020)

In 2020, mRNA vaccines were developed. Pfizer-BioNTech. Moderna. Based on Karikó's research. Saved millions.

### Nobel Prize (2023)

In 2023, Karikó won the Nobel Prize in Physiology or Medicine with Drew Weissman.

## 4. THE GRIND — The Invisible Years (The Method)
