# Jack Dorsey — The Founder Who Was Fired Twice from Twitter and Came Back

## 1. THE HOOK

In 2006, he cofounded Twitter. In 2008, he was fired as CEO. In 2009, he cofounded Square (now Block). In 2015, he returned as Twitter CEO. In 2021, he left Twitter again. In 2021, Square rebranded to Block. He says: "Twitter is about the small stuff."

How does a founder get fired twice and come back?

## 2. ORIGIN — THE "Before" (The Relatability Engine)

Jack Dorsey was born November 19, 1976, in St. Louis, Missouri. He studied at New York University (dropped out), University of Missouri-Rolla (transferred).

He was running from traditional education. He saw messaging. He wanted to build communication tools.

## 3. THE FIRE — Failures, Rock-Bottom & Turning Points

### Twitter (2006)

In 2006, Dorsey cofounded Twitter with Noah Glass, Biz Stone, Evan Williams. 140 characters. Tweets.

### CEO (2007-2008)

Dorsey was CEO from 2007-2008. He was fired. "Caused problems."

### Square (2009-Present)

In 2009, Dorsey cofounded Square. Payment processing. Changed name to Block in 2021.

### Twitter CEO Return (2015-2021)

In 2015, Dorsey returned as Twitter CEO. He led until 2021.

### Bitcoin Focus

Square bought $50M Bitcoin in 2020, $170M more in 2021. Cash App. Bitcoin.

### Twitter Departure (2021)

In 2021, Dorsey left Twitter again. Parag Agrawal became CEO.

## 4. THE GRIND — The Invisible Years (The Method)

Dorsey's method is defined by small stuff, purpose, and meditation.

### Small Stuff

Dorsey's philosophy: "Twitter is about the small stuff." Small messages matter.

### Purpose

Dorsey's philosophy: "I think the most important thing is to have a really strong sense of purpose."

### Meditation

Dorsey meditates. Silent Twitter board meetings. Focus.

The invisible years—the years after Twitter firing—gave Dorsey Square. He returned to Twitter.

## 5. THE TEST — THE BREAKTHROUGH MOMENT

Square is the test. Twitter cofounded, fired, returned, left again. Resilience works.

## 6. THE PHILOSOPHY — Stealable Wisdom

**On Twitter:**
"Twitter is about the small stuff."

**On Startups:**
"Every single person who starts a company goes through a lot of tough times."

**On Purpose:**

## 7. THE LEGACY — Purpose Beyond Profit + Human Side

Jack Dorsey is CEO of Block. He focuses on Bitcoin. He mediates.

### What does he want to be remembered for?

In interviews, Dorsey emphasizes purpose and empowerment. He wants to empower small businesses.
