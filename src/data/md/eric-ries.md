# Eric Ries — The Creator of Lean Startup

## 1. THE HOOK

In 2004, he cofounded IMVU. It struggled. He learned from failure. In 2011, he wrote "The Lean Startup." It sold over 1 million copies. It was translated into 30+ languages. He created the Build-Measure-Learn loop. He popularized Minimum Viable Product (MVP). He influenced Steve Blank, agile development. He says: "The only way to win is to learn faster than anyone else."

How does a failed founder transform how startups are built?

## 2. ORIGIN — THE "Before" (The Relatability Engine)

Eric Ries was born in 1978 or 1979. He studied at Yale. He became an entrepreneur.

He was running from conventional careers. He saw technology. He understood startups.

## 3. THE FIRE — Failures, Rock-Bottom & Turning Points

### IMVU (2004)

Ries cofounded IMVU, a social gaming platform. The company struggled. Ries learned from failure.

### The Lean Startup (2011)

In 2011, Ries wrote "The Lean Startup." It sold 1M+ copies. It was translated into 30+ languages. It transformed startup methodology.

### The Startup Way (2017)

In 2017, Ries wrote "The Startup Way." He applied Lean Startup to enterprises.

## 4. THE GRIND — The Invisible Years (The Method)

Ries's method is defined by Build-Measure-Learn, MVP, and validated learning.

### Build-Measure-Learn

Ries's loop: Build. Measure. Learn. Iterate rapidly.

### Minimum Viable Product (MVP)

Ries's concept: Ship early. Learn fast. Do not build the perfect product first.

### Validated Learning

Ries's philosophy: Evidence-based progress. Do not assume. Test.

The invisible years—the years of IMVU struggle—taught Ries what doesn't work. He systematized learning.

## 5. THE TEST — The Breakthrough Moment

The Lean Startup is the test. 1M+ copies sold. 30+ languages. It transformed entrepreneurship education worldwide.

## 6. THE PHILOSOPHY — Stealable Wisdom

**On Learning:**
"The only way to win is to learn faster than anyone else."

**On Customers:**
"We must learn what customers really want, not what they say they want or what we think they should want."

**On Steering:**
"The Lean Startup method teaches you how to steer your business with maximum acceleration."

## 7. THE LEGACY — Purpose Beyond Profit + Human Side

Eric Ries is still active. He writes. He teaches. He speaks.

### What does he want to be remembered for?

In interviews, Ries emphasizes validated learning and speed. He wants founders to test assumptions.

## 8. FINAL MOTIVATION & PERSONAL APPLICATION Guide

Eric Ries's career demonstrates that methodology transforms startups.

3. **Validate learning** — Evidence-based progress. Do not assume.

4. **Pivot or persevere** — Make strategic decisions based on data.

5. **Learn faster** — "The only way to win is to learn faster than anyone else."

If Ries transformed startups through validated learning—are you testing or assuming?

Methodology transforms startups.
