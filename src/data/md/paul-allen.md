# Paul Allen — The Idea Man Who Cofounded Microsoft

## 1. THE HOOK

In 1975, he cofounded Microsoft with Bill Gates. He envisioned the "wired world" before the internet. He left Microsoft in 1983 after Hodgkin's lymphoma diagnosis. He became a billionaire investor. He donated $2B+ to science. He says: "I wanted to be involved in the creation of the future."

How does a dropout from Washington State become a visionary investor?

## 2. ORIGIN — THE "Before" (The Relatability Engine)

Paul Allen was born January 21, 1953, in Seattle, Washington. He studied at Washington State University (dropped out).

He was running from traditional education. He saw personal computing. He wanted to build the future.

## 3. THE FIRE — Failures, Rock-Bottom & Turning Points

### Microsoft (1975-1983)

In 1975, Allen cofounded Microsoft with Bill Gates. He envisioned the "wired world."

### Hodgkin's Lymphoma (1983)

In 1983, Allen was diagnosed with Hodgkin's lymphoma. He left Microsoft. He recovered.

### Investor (1983-2018)

Allen founded Vulcan Inc. He invested in real estate, sports teams (Seattle Seahawks, Portland Trail Blazers, Seattle Sounders).

### Philanthropy

Allen founded Allen Institute for Brain Science, Allen Institute for Artificial Intelligence, Allen Institute for Cell Science. Donated $2B+ to science and arts. Signed Giving Pledge 2010.

### Death (2018)

Allen died October 15, 2018, at 65.

## 4. THE GRIND — The Invisible Years (The Method)

Allen's method is defined by vision, investing, and philanthropy.

### Vision

Allen's philosophy: "I wanted to be involved in the creation of the future." Wired world.

### Investing

Allen's philosophy: "The best investments I've made are the ones that didn't make sense to a lot of people." Contrarian.

### Philanthropy

Allen's philosophy: Science and arts. Allen Institutes. Give back.

The invisible years—the years after Microsoft—gave Allen investment opportunities. He funded science.
