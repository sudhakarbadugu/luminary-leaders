# Yann LeCun — The Optimist Who Built Computer Vision

## 1. THE HOOK

In 2018, he won the Turing Award with Geoffrey Hinton and Yoshua Bengio. He is the Chief AI Scientist at Meta. He is a professor at NYU. He pioneered convolutional neural networks (CNNs). He built LeNet for digit recognition in the 1990s. It read checks. He developed the Torch deep learning framework. He advocates for self-supervised learning. He is an AI optimist. He says: "AI is not going to take over the world anytime soon."

How does a French scientist build the foundation for computer vision?

## 2. ORIGIN — THE "Before" (The Relatability Engine)

Yann LeCun was born in July 1960 in the suburbs of Paris, France. He studied in France. He moved to the US.

He was running from traditional AI. He believed in neural networks. He built CNNs.

## 3. THE FIRE — Failures, Rock-Bottom & Turning Points

### Convolutional Neural Networks

LeCun pioneered CNNs in the 1980s and 1990s. He showed how neural networks could learn visual representations.

### LeNet

LeCun built LeNet—an early CNN for digit recognition. It was used commercially in the 1990s for reading checks.

### Torch

LeCun developed Torch—an early deep learning framework. It influenced PyTorch.

### Turing Award (2018)

In 2018, LeCun won the Turing Award with Hinton and Bengio. They were recognized for deep learning breakthroughs.

### Meta (Facebook)

LeCun is Chief AI Scientist at Meta. He leads AI research.

## 4. THE GRIND — The Invisible Years (The Method)

LeCun's method is defined by CNNs, self-supervised learning, and open source.

### CNNs

LeCun's breakthrough: convolutional neural networks. They learn hierarchical representations from images.

### Self-Supervised Learning

LeCun advocates for self-supervised learning. AI learns from observation, like humans and animals.

### Open Source

LeCun believes in open source AI. "Open source is the only way forward for AI."

The invisible years—the years of CNN research—were years of conviction. LeCun built what others thought impossible.

## 5. THE TEST — The Breakthrough Moment

LeNet is the test. It worked commercially. CNNs became the foundation of computer vision.
