# Biz Stone — The Twitter Cofounder Who Believes Creativity Is Renewable

## 1. THE HOOK

In 2006, he cofounded Twitter. In 2010, he founded Jelly. He worked at Google on Blogger. He designed the Twitter logo. He left Twitter in 2011. He says: "The most powerful thing you can do is be yourself."

How does a dropout from Northeastern cofound Twitter?

## 2. ORIGIN — THE "Before" (The Relatability Engine)

Biz Stone was born 1974 in Philadelphia, Pennsylvania. He studied at Northeastern University (dropped out), University of Massachusetts Boston.

He was running from traditional education. He saw social media. He wanted to connect people.

## 3. THE FIRE — Failures, Rock-Bottom & Turning Points

### Google (2003-2006)

Stone worked at Google on Blogger. After Google acquired Pyra Labs.

### Twitter (2006-2011)

In 2006, Stone cofounded Twitter with Jack Dorsey, Evan Williams, Noah Glass.

### Twitter Logo

Stone helped design the Twitter logo. The blue bird.

### Jelly Industries (2010-2014)

In 2010, Stone founded Jelly Industries. Q&A app. Sold to Pinterest in 2014.

### Investor and Advisor

Stone invests and advises startups. Medium investor.
